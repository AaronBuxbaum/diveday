# Rules for `src/i18n/`

The **i18n-copy** skill is the procedure. Each rule names what enforces it; the reasoning is in
[docs/agents/repo-checks.md](../../docs/agents/repo-checks.md#the-i18n-rules).

- **Bundles**: diver words in `locales/<locale>/diver.json`; staff words in
  `locales/<locale>/staff/<namespace>.json`, one file per area, composed by `staff-messages.ts`
  (ADR 20260807-per-area-staff-bundles). `staffTranslator` is server-side only; staff Client
  Components take words as props.
- **Every key lands in every locale in the same change**, and a delete is three edits (call site,
  `en-US`, `es-ES`): `pnpm check:locale`.
- **Spanish**: read `src/i18n/locales/es-ES/README.md` first.
- **Locale resolution**: the `diveday_locale` cookie (`src/i18n/locale-cookie.ts`), then
  `Accept-Language`, then `shops.default_locale`; the switcher is one Server Action
  (`src/app/actions/set-locale.ts`); no `[locale]` route (ADR 20260812-reader-chosen-language).
- **A diver Client Component that reads copy has `DiverIntlProvider` above it** with full
  `namespaces`: `src/i18n/provider-coverage.test.ts`.
- **No bundle reads as machine-written** (prose em dash, intensifiers, "Here's how", "not just X",
  "No X. No Y.") and English spells American (H-95): `pnpm check:voice`, per locale. Reasoning in
  [docs/design/brand.md](../../docs/design/brand.md)'s "What gives us away".
- **The apostrophe is `’` (U+2019)**, except ICU quoting (`'{depth18}'`): `pnpm check:voice`
  (issue #1367).
- **Vocabulary the guards refuse**: Archive/Deactivate/soft-delete (the `softDelete` lint rule,
  ADR 20260820-every-delete-is-soft), "shop" where the entity word is decided otherwise
  (`scripts/check-shop-word.mjs`), ICU plurals missing a category (`scripts/check-icu-plurals.mjs`).
- **Words that are DiveDay's, not a shop's** come from their label modules (`marine-life-labels.ts`,
  `readiness-labels.ts`, `dive-site-labels.ts`, `buddy-labels.ts`, `gear-labels.ts`), never spelled
  inline.
- **Every sentence earns its place**: the **copy-restraint** skill.
- **Waiver and medical wording stays English** pending H-01/H-03; its dates are still
  locale-negotiated.
