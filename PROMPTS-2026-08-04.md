# Overnight improvement prompts — 2026-08-04

Eleven self-contained prompts, each meant to be pasted into its own Fable session and run in
parallel. Each carries its own goal, an explicit Opus-subagent delegation plan, and a "Done when"
contract the session must loop against until every item is true — ending with a ready-for-review
PR on its own branch.

**Composition philosophy.** Nothing here is a lift of an already-specced backlog task — the
prompt-ready entries in `docs/product/features/ai-ml.md` and the mechanical chores will happen on
their own schedule. This sheet spends the overnight fleet on the things only a big model with a
whole night can do: **big bets** the brainstorm has been circling for weeks, **concepts nailed
down** so hard they become executable, and **innovation** on the moments that decide whether a
shop signs up. Nothing requires AI/ML at runtime, and nothing touches the human-owned H-/V- gates
(`docs/product/human-decisions.md`).

## Run notes (for the human dispatching these)

- **Parallel-safe by design**: each prompt owns disjoint primary paths and declares them in its PR
  description. The unavoidable shared hotspots are append-heavy files — `src/i18n/locales/*` JSON,
  `e2e/visual.spec.ts`, `scripts/route-coverage.json`, `src/db/schema.ts` (prompts 1–3), and
  `src/db/seed.ts`'s one-line orchestrator — where conflicts will be trivial "both added a block"
  merges. Prompts 9 and 10 are mostly test-additive and instructed to keep product-code fixes
  minimal and coordinated.
- **Merge order doesn't matter**, with one nuance: if Prompt 11's new gates fail after another PR
  merges, the failing addition violates a rule that already exists in prose — fix the addition,
  not the gate.
- Prompt 8 deliberately ships **no code** — it's a design nail-down for the repo's most avoided
  modeling question. Judge it as a decision document.

## Prompt 2 — Group trips: claim-your-seat links
