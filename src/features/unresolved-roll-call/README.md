# Unresolved roll call

The internal recovery lookup rechecks active staff roles and scopes a stored
observation to its shop, trip and client event. It is not a handoff receipt or
review decision. Capture remains disabled until retention, authenticated
review, lifecycle, and safe receipt handling are complete; callers must retain
the device's pending evidence. A same-origin, no-store staff endpoint is disabled pending intent matching and erasure-fenced authorization. A lookup must not acknowledge delivery, settle the event, or resolve the observation. There is no review decision UI yet.

The disabled delivery tick can report aggregate ledger problems for operations.
It deliberately reports attention required until a durable scan-progress and
recipient frontier exists; a completed scan or provider acceptance does not
prove that every current recipient was notified. The aggregate is internal,
not a tenant-scoped reader. Escalation resolution, bounded monitoring at scale,
and the production adapter remain prerequisites for enabling the workflow.
