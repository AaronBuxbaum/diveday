# Switching pages: outcome first, migration second

## Current direction

Aaron requested Peek as a competitor and a full review of the switching pages on 2026-11-11.
The old pages mostly sold an export procedure. Their generic claims about competitors and
fixed migration estimates were stronger than the evidence.

The hub now asks the reader to choose the workflow they want to improve. Each guide opens
with its own practical reason to compare, then demonstrates the existing Today screen before
asking the reader to prepare files. The demonstration is explicitly a sample, not customer
proof. It opens the owner demo on Today and links to the certification and manifest pages.
The incumbent's useful capabilities and reasons to retain it are stated directly.

The six competitor guides are FareHarbor, Peek Pro, EVE, DiveShop360, Smartwaiver and Rezdy.
The spreadsheet guide shares the same proof section. Checkfront was researched as a claim
cross-check, not added as an unrequested seventh competitor.

## Evidence and limits, checked 2026-11-11

- **Peek Pro:** [rentals](https://www.peekpro.com/rentals) and
  [revenue features](https://www.peekpro.com/product/grow-revenue) document waivers, resources,
  booking and distribution. The [customer portal](https://support.peek.com/hc/en-us/articles/29197460170388-Using-Your-Customer-Portal)
  has operator-dependent options. No public export click path or fixed file schema was verified.
  The guide is explicitly a sample-review plan: the operator checks available exports with Peek
  and obtains their own files. It promises no automated Peek connection or guaranteed scope.
- **EVE:** the [sales module](https://evediving.com/page/sales_module) documents spreadsheet
  exports; the [courses module](https://evediving.com/page/courses_module) documents course and
  PADI work. A universal customer/certification export menu was not verified. Removed claims
  about one PC, difficult history extraction and support producing a particular file on demand.
- **DiveShop360:** the [FAQ](https://diveshop360.com/faq) confirms customer, inventory, sales and
  certification exports, cloud access and dive-shop workflows. It does not verify the assumed
  menu, schema or universal absence of an API. The guide asks to inspect the account's files.
- **Smartwaiver:** [Participant CSV](https://support.smartwaiver.com/hc/en-us/articles/360056592012-How-do-I-export-my-completed-waiver-data)
  has a documented Export Data path. [Signed PDFs](https://support.smartwaiver.com/hc/en-us/articles/360057047891-Can-I-download-copies-of-my-signed-waivers-as-PDFs)
  are separate. The guide no longer promises nobody must sign again or implies the CSV is the
  signed document. Legal validity, release version and medical evidence need review by the shop.
- **FareHarbor:** the [Contacts report](https://help.fareharbor.com/hc/en-us/articles/40898088413595-Contacts-report)
  documents the report path, permission, download and cancellation-filter behavior. Preserved
  the source-specific transfer scope and manual upcoming-booking work. Removed reported fee and
  hosted-website price figures. Replacing embeds does not transfer a hosted website.
- **Rezdy:** [Sales–Orders](https://support.rezdy.com/hc/en-us/articles/19867797356316-How-To-Use-the-Sales-Orders-Report)
  documents CSV export. The [customer list](https://support.rezdy.com/hc/en-us/articles/19867758531612-Customers-Mutual-Guests)
  represents the main booking contact, not necessarily each diver. Future departures and
  participant details need separate preparation.
- **Cross-check only:** [Checkfront's scuba page](https://www.checkfront.com/scuba-diving-booking-software/)
  advertises dive resources and medical/waiver workflows. Category-level assertions that a
  booking system cannot handle dive operations are not safe claims.

No competitor price or savings estimate is needed to make these guides useful. Current account
terms, payment processing, distribution value and DiveDay's configured offer determine the
comparison. The guides link to pricing and keep the same copy for every configured offer.

## Migration boundaries

`IMPORT_HONESTY_TABLE` and its localized rows remain unchanged. They describe importer support,
not the columns any competitor promises to export. Source-specific text makes that distinction
before the reference. Historical visits do not create future bookings or seats. Parallel use
requires staff to check new bookings, changes and cancellations. Re-importing contacts is not
booking synchronization. Shops keep original documents and receipts.

The fixed afternoon estimate is removed from the shared cutover copy, including its homepage
reuse. A sample and one trip trial establish the work before selecting a date. Free personal
migration assistance remains the existing authorized offer. Website work remains an invitation
to discuss needs, with no price or delivery promise.

## Review changes

The independent conversion review removed the four-fact hero grid, which repeated the later
import and assistance sections before showing the product. The first CTA now carries the
configured offer and shared demo terms. FareHarbor uses one keep-or-move decision section,
not the same choice twice. Peek’s export uncertainty is stated in operator language with a
practical request for a sample, rather than repeated research disclaimers. The domain review
confirmed that manual live-booking work and imported evidence remain distinct from a
booking connection or a new signature.
