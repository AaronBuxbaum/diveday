# H-64: Which direction from the 2026-09-01 canvas does DiveDay wear, and whose brand do the…

- **Status:** Chosen
- **Human owner:** Product owner

## Decision or approval needed

Which direction from the 2026-09-01 canvas does DiveDay wear, and whose brand do the diver-facing surfaces carry? Three directions for DiveDay's own face (Tide, Deck, Reef) and one axis for the diver's screen (Harbor: the shop's brand, not DiveDay's) were drawn on [the canvas](../../design/canvases/20260901-diveday-reimagined/README.md) and argued in ADR [20260901-diveday-reimagined](../../architecture/decisions/20260901-diveday-reimagined.md).

## Minimum outcome to record

A named direction for the staff app, a yes or no on Harbor, and a yes or no on the embed catalogue the canvas proposed.

## Unblocks / follow-up

**Decided 2026-09-01 (Aaron Buxbaum, in session):** the staff app wears **Reef** — but with **Geist kept as the only face**; Fraunces is declined because it moves the brand voice too far. Every diver-facing surface goes to **Harbor**, wearing whatever brand settings the shop has elected to use. The embed catalogue and the brand settings the canvas proposed are approved as drawn. A **hosted website, built to order** joins the offer as an authorized service (see H-65). The ADR is Accepted with those decisions; slices 13a–13j in the roadmap.
