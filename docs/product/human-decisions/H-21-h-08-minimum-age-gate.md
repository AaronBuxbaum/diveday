# H-21: H-08's minimum-age gate (people.date_of_birth, 20260724-gear-fit-fallback) made age a piece of data DiveDay now holds…

- **Status:** Implemented
- **Human owner:** Product owner + dive operations lead

## Decision or approval needed

H-08's minimum-age gate (`people.date_of_birth`, [20260724-gear-fit-fallback](../../architecture/decisions/20260724-gear-fit-fallback.md)) made age a piece of data DiveDay now holds and enforces, but it surfaces nowhere a crew actually looks: the roster, the manifest, and roll call show no age or minor indicator, so a captain reading the boarding list has no way to know a booked diver is 12 without opening their profile. Separately, and only reachable now that the concept of a minor exists in the product at all: a minor can sign their own liability waiver through the ordinary `/waivers/[token]` flow — there is no guardian-signature step, no co-signer field, and no block on a minor completing it solo. Does the roster/manifest need an age or minor indicator, and does the waiver flow need a guardian-signature path for a diver under a jurisdiction's age of majority (or under the agency's junior-certification threshold, 15)?

## Minimum outcome to record

Whether to surface age/minor status on staff-facing rosters and manifests (and where), and whether/how the waiver flow requires a parent/guardian signature for a minor — a design (co-signer capture, a distinct minor waiver template, a staff-witnessed paper fallback) or a deliberate deferral with the current solo-signature behavior accepted as-is pending H-01–H-03's broader waiver legal review.

## Unblocks / follow-up

**Decided 2026-07-30 (Aaron Buxbaum):** the roster surfaces age in years (when a date of birth is on file) and a minor indicator. Also authorized in the same decision: a celebratory birthday callout — a section similar to the existing dive-count milestone (e.g. "25 dives") that shows when a diver's birthday is today or coming up soon. **Solo-signature is accepted as-is for now** — explicitly called out (not silently left open) so it comes back for review alongside the broader H-01–H-03 waiver legal work rather than being forgotten. **Shipped 2026-07-30** ([20260730-site-depth-and-diver-age-surfaces](../../architecture/decisions/20260730-site-depth-and-diver-age-surfaces.md)): the roster and the boat manifest carry age in years, a **Minor** badge (18 — legal majority, the guardian-signature threshold; the diving world's 15 is handled separately by the junior depth bands), and a birthday badge, with a `Celebrations` section above the roster. The owner set the window at **seven days in either direction** and the copy at icon-plus-timing only ("🎂 in 2d" / "🎂 2d ago"). The "existing dive-count milestone" this row referred to does not exist in the codebase, so the callout's shape was confirmed with the owner rather than guessed. **Solo minor signature closed 2026-09-07 (Aaron Buxbaum, improvement-ideas decision sheet, item N-38 — *Build now*), shipped the same day** ([20260907-guardian-co-signature](../../architecture/decisions/20260907-guardian-co-signature.md)): a diver who is a minor on the shop-local day they sign gets a second card on the same `/waivers/[token]` page — a parent or legal guardian's full name, a relationship code, an email and their own consent box, captured under the same typed-consent provider as the diver's own signature. Readiness raises a distinct `guardian_signature_missing` blocker until both signatures exist, the diver record reads that record as its own state rather than **Signed**, the integrity seal covers the co-signature, and the paper-release form on the roster, the counter and the diver record asks the staffer who countersigned. The rule still fails open where no date of birth is on file, as H-08's minimum-age gate does. **This does not close H-01–H-03**: the release text is unchanged and still English, and whether typed consent is a sufficient assurance level is H-03's question for the guardian exactly as it is for the diver.
