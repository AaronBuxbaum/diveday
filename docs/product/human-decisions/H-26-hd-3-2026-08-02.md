## Unblocks / follow-up

**Decided 2026-08-02 (Aaron Buxbaum): DiveDay is a deliberately bounded, founder-run lifestyle business**, not a venture-scale trajectory — no outside capital, no aggressive multi-shop growth target. The existing $99 flat price, no-platform-fee stance, 25-shop founding cohort cap, and two-year price lock (H-12) are consistent with that choice and stay unchanged; [vision.md](../vision.md) now states the choice explicitly instead of leaving it implicit. **Support-promise language loosened in the same decision (owner's instruction: "keep it ambiguous for now"):** the published "founder-direct, same-day response" support commitment drops the explicit same-day SLA — now "founder-direct support" — across `marketing.ts`, the pricing/home page copy (`diver.json`), `marketing.md`'s claims policy, `roadmap.md`'s pricing-posture gate, and `legal.md`/`legal-engagement-scope.md`'s list of binding commercial commitments the SaaS contract set must encode. The founder-direct-line commitment itself (a real person, not a ticket queue) is unchanged; only the explicit time-bound guarantee is removed, pending a real answer on support-hour capacity. Whether one person can sustainably support the founding cohort at its eventual size, and at what hour budget, remains open. **Superseded 2026-08-05 (H-12 row):** rather than answer that capacity question, the owner retired the founder-direct-support promise itself — see H-12's amendment above. Founding-cohort price lock (H-12) proceeds without a pricing rework; the SaaS contract set's support language (H-18's contract set, H-12's binding terms) should encode the plain `support@dive.day` support commitment, not a founder-direct or same-day guarantee.
