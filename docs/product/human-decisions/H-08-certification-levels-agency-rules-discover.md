# H-08: Which certification levels, agency rules, and Discover Scuba Diver rules define a course booking?

- **Status:** In progress
- **Human owner:** Product owner + operations lead

## Decision or approval needed

Which certification levels, agency rules, and Discover Scuba Diver rules define a course booking? Separately: the glossary's own **C-card** entry states real certification cards do not expire — should the `certifications`/`specialty_certifications` "expiry" field be relabeled and presented to staff as a shop-set "refresher due" date instead of an implied card expiry (see [20260723-certification-expiry-date-only](../../architecture/decisions/20260723-certification-expiry-date-only.md), flagged by a dive-domain-expert review of CR-009)?

## Minimum outcome to record

Supported courses, prerequisites, expiration/verification rules, ratios, and exception process. Plus: whether/how to relabel the expiry field and its staff-facing copy.

## Unblocks / follow-up

**Relabel decided + shipped 2026-07-24, and superseded 2026-08-21 — the concept is gone, not renamed:** the relabel presented the optional card date to staff as a shop-set **"refresher due"** date and left the stored column, the gating and the readiness codes untouched, so the strictest gate in the product went on enforcing a rule no agency has. The product owner reversed that half on **2026-08-21** (issue #630, combining #626 and #609): `certifications.expires_at` and `specialty_certifications.expires_at` are **dropped**, along with the `certification_expired` / `specialty_expired` blockers, the depth-ceiling and course-prerequisite checks that read them, every form field and rendered line, and the CSV `certification_expires_at` mapping. Boarding is strictly *more* permissive as a result, which is the intent — see [20260821-a-card-does-not-expire](../../architecture/decisions/20260821-a-card-does-not-expire.md), which supersedes the certification-expiry ADR. Untouched by that removal: `waiver_records.expires_at` (a waiver really does lapse), the Scuba Refresher **course** template (a real product), and dive recency (`bookings.last_dived_band`), which is the question that actually catches people ([20260821-currency-is-what-catches-people](../../architecture/decisions/20260821-currency-is-what-catches-people.md)). The same pass capped the levels a **site or trip may require** at Rescue: Divemaster and Instructor stay valid on a held card and still count for crew and course ratios, but a shop may not demand a working rating of a paying diver. **Course baseline approved as the starting point 2026-07-24 (Aaron Buxbaum):** the [conservative provisional rules](README.md#course-admission) — DSD/OW ungated, AOW/refresher requiring a verified Open Water card, instructor-led sessions blocked until an instructor is assigned — are accepted as the operating baseline. **Ratios resolved + shipped 2026-07-24 (Aaron Buxbaum: use PADI/SSI's own published standards directly, consistent enough across agencies to trust):** the entry-level (DSD/Open Water) in-water ratio — 8 students per instructor, +2 per certified assistant, ceiling 12 — is now a real booking gate on any ungated course session (`src/lib/course-ratios.ts`, [20260724-course-admission-standards](../../architecture/decisions/20260724-course-admission-standards.md)), sourced and cited there; continuing-ed courses aren't ratio-capped, matching PADI's own published scope. **Minimum age resolved + shipped 2026-07-24 (Aaron Buxbaum chose option B — collect a date of birth, fail open):** `people.date_of_birth` is now an optional diver-profile field, and a course's `minimum_age` is enforced at booking **only when a date is on file**, measured on the day the course runs rather than the day it's booked. A diver with no date books exactly as before, so shipping it changed nothing for existing divers — option C (fail closed) was declined for exactly that blast radius, and stays available as a later upgrade once shops have backfilled. See [20260724-gear-fit-fallback](../../architecture/decisions/20260724-gear-fit-fallback.md). **Depth ceilings: decided 2026-07-30 (Aaron Buxbaum), shipped the same day** ([20260730-site-depth-and-diver-age-surfaces](../../architecture/decisions/20260730-site-depth-and-diver-age-surfaces.md)). `dive_sites` gets a structured numeric depth field (replacing/supplementing the free-text `depth_range`). When a site's depth exceeds what a diver's certification level supports (including the Junior 12 m/18 m/21 m bands), staff see a **warning, not a block** — an instructor may deliberately keep a diver shallower than the site's max, so the system flags rather than refuses. As built: `dive_sites.max_depth_meters` (canonical metres) versus `src/lib/depth-ceiling.ts`'s ceilings, surfaced as `depthAdvisory` — a sibling of readiness that can never become a blocker — over the deepest site the trip visits. A `shops.depth_unit` setting lets a shop read metres or feet without changing what is stored. **Medical-exception process:** covered by the existing waiver medical-questionnaire physician-referral gate (H-01–H-03 territory); no separate course-level exception process needed. **DSD ratio corrected 2026-08-02 (Aaron Buxbaum, HD-6 — real PADI Instructor Manual figures):** DSD had been held to the Open Water training ratio (8→12:1) for lack of a real DSD number. The Instructor Manual figure is **4 students per instructor in confined/pool water, 2 students per instructor for the open-water dive** — both tighter than what was enforced. `entryLevelCourseCapacity` now takes `courses.isIntroCourse` and applies the DSD figure (2:1 open water — the only one DiveDay's trip model can enforce, since it has no confined-water session type; no assistant-bonus credit, none is published) instead of the Open Water figure for a DSD session. Open Water training capacity is unchanged. See [20260802-dsd-instructor-manual-ratio](../../architecture/decisions/20260802-dsd-instructor-manual-ratio.md). Pending `dive-domain-expert` review before merge (safety-critical). **Ratio scope, undo, and crew-change defects fixed alongside HD-6 (Engineering, review findings DOM-H2/DATA-L2 in [comprehensive-review-20260802](../archive/comprehensive-review-20260802.md)):** the corrected 2:1 DSD figure now applies to **every agency**, not only PADI — the reason it is tighter (participants with zero prior water time) does not depend on whose logo is on the course, and scoping it to PADI left an SSI/NAUI Try Scuba capped by nothing but the boat's capacity. The PADI check stays on the cited 8/+2/12 entry-level figure only. An intro session also stays gated even if a `minimum_certification_level` was typed onto it; `courses.agency` comparisons are normalized (a shop that typed `"PADI"` previously lost the cap entirely, DATA-L2); the "which ratio does this session carry" predicate has one definition (`courseRatioKind`) instead of three inline copies; `restoreBooking` re-checks the ratio so an Undo cannot seat an extra first-timer; and `setTripCrew`/`changeTripCrew` **record** a crew change that leaves a session over ratio rather than refusing it, so the manifest never lists crew who are not aboard (the gap surfaces as the non-blocking `over_ratio` advisory; new bookings stay refused). See the [amendment](../../architecture/decisions/20260724-course-admission-standards.md#amendment-2026-08-02--intro-session-ratio-scope-undo-and-crew-changes). **Still open from HD-6:** the Rescue Diver scenario-supervision figure.
