# H-30: Even after the 2026-08-03 copy fix, the public booking form's succeed/fail signal is a…

- **Status:** Chosen
- **Human owner:** Product owner + `security-reviewer`

## Decision or approval needed

Even after the 2026-08-03 copy fix, the **public booking form's succeed/fail signal is a certification-level oracle**. The refusal text now describes only the trip (identical for every submitter, revealing only what the page above the form already displays), but *that a submission failed at all* still tells an anonymous caller that the person on file for a guessed email does not hold what the trip demands — and a handful of probes across trips at different levels bracket that person's certification level. H-22 ruled that the public form never refuses on a personal attribute (which is why `course_min_age` and `course_prerequisite` collapse to a non-disclosing "unavailable"), and this refusal is one. Options: collapse it back to the generic sentence (restores the oracle-free posture, and restores the "isn't taking bookings" statement on a page showing spots left); keep it and accept the oracle as the price of an honest refusal; or admit the seat publicly and let readiness catch it, refusing only at staff doors.

## Minimum outcome to record

Whether the public booking form may refuse on the trip's certification gate at all, and if so whether the oracle is accepted.

## Unblocks / follow-up

**Decided 2026-08-20: keep the refusal, accept the oracle** (option 2). The owner is not concerned by the attack: bracketing a guessed diver's rung takes a handful of probes across trips and yields a certification level, on a platform whose refusal already names the trip's requirement publicly. Weighed against that, option 1 tells an honest customer the trip "isn't taking bookings" on a page showing four spots left — a lie to everybody, to deny a rare prober a fact of low value. Option 3 (sell the seat, refuse at the dock) trades the oracle for a refund conversation and a diver who finds out at the water. **H-27 also narrows this considerably**: now that the form collects and believes what the diver says, the common refusal is a response to what this submitter just typed rather than a disclosure about somebody on file. No code change — `bookSpot`'s `trip_prerequisite` arm stays on the public path. Directly extends H-22; interacts with H-27. **Amended 2026-08-20 along the line this row itself draws:** the refusal now stands down for exactly the case described above — a *level* shortfall resting on the rung **this submitter just typed** — which is answered by a warning under their own select instead, and which discloses nothing about anybody on file because the submitter supplied the only fact it reads. Every other refusal is untouched, including the one this row was actually about: a submission carrying **no declaration**, refused over the record, still refuses. That is the case where option 3's "refund conversation and a diver who finds out at the water" applies, and it was the reason to keep the stop. Specialty and nitrox shortfalls also keep it, since no field on the form can answer either. Rides with FU-20260820-the-sale-gate-bites-only-the-honest's resolution.
