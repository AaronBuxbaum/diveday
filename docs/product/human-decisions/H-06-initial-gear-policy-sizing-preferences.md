# H-06: What is the initial gear policy for sizing, preferences, staff assignment, and substitutions?

- **Status:** Implemented
- **Human owner:** Product owner + dive operations lead

## Decision or approval needed

What is the initial gear policy for sizing, preferences, staff assignment, and substitutions?

## Minimum outcome to record

Required measurements/preferences, who may override a request, and the safe fallback when a size is unavailable.

## Unblocks / follow-up

**Chosen 2026-07-24 (default-kit membership), refined 2026-07-24 (pricing):** the **dive computer defaults on for every diver** and is in the default rental catalog, but is **priced on its own line, not folded into the "set" bundle** (`src/lib/rentals.ts`; the GoPro is likewise a default-off add-on). The owner's first cut folded it into the set; on review that was reversed so a diver who brings their own computer keeps the full-set discount on the five hard-goods pieces — being on by default is separate from being part of the priced set. Shipped with tests and the [rental gear request](README.md#rental-gear-request) note updated. **Measurement method decided 2026-07-24 (Aaron Buxbaum):** the booking rental request captures self-reported BCD/wetsuit size, boot/fin size, and usual weighting as a **planning input only** — never a reservation or fit authorization — with staff confirming true fit at check-in (already the shape of the [rental gear request](README.md#rental-gear-request)). **Substitution authority + safe fallback: decided, in implementation** — any staff member on the trip may substitute a real available item (logged), but *overriding/denying* a diver's stated request is reserved to instructor/divemaster/manager (rides on the H-14 role matrix), and when a requested size is unavailable the request drops to a **"needs staff fit"** state rather than auto-assigning a different size. **Implemented 2026-07-24:** the override gate is `canOverrideGearRequest` (owner/manager/instructor/**divemaster** — wider than `canConfigureTrips` on purpose, since sizing a diver is in-water judgement), enforced in both layers per ADR-0006; the fallback is a first-class **"needs staff fit"** state on `rental_fit_profiles`. The flagged diver keeps their line and count on the prep list — the count is what the packer loads from, so dropping it arrives a BCD short with nothing to fit them from — but their *sized* pieces carry no size, reading "fit at check-in", because packing a substitute is the thing it prevents. Unsized kit (regulator, computer, GoPro), weights, and tanks are untouched: none has a stock size to be short of. They are named in their own check-in section along with the sizes they asked for, and the flag is sticky against size edits. **Raising** the flag stays open to every staff member — it escalates to a person rather than overwriting the diver's request — while **clearing** it takes the override gate, since that asserts the stated size packs after all. Recording a diver's *first* fit is open too: there is nothing on file to override. See [20260724-gear-fit-fallback](../../architecture/decisions/20260724-gear-fit-fallback.md). *(This decision arrived labelled "H-05" but its content — the default kit — belongs to this gear-policy row; register H-05 is the offline-manifest thresholds.)* **Rental-set pricing re-confirmed 2026-08-02 (Aaron Buxbaum, HD-9 — code/tests vs. decision-log/glossary contradiction):** the dive computer **stays in** `CORE_RENTAL_KINDS`/the priced "set" (matching shipped code and tests, reversing the 2026-07-24 pricing-refinement note above that had said it should be priced separately) — but a diver who skips a core piece (brings their own dive computer, say) is never charged more than the full set costs: `quoteRentalFit` now bills whichever is cheaper, the set price or the sum of the pieces actually rented, instead of always dropping to per-piece pricing on anything short of the full set. See `src/lib/rentals.ts`, updated tests, and [`docs/product/glossary.md`](../glossary.md#operations).
