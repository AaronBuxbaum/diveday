## Original question

**How is the built-to-order website priced, and what may the pages promise about it?** H-20260901-canvas-direction-brand added it to the offer: a shop asks, and DiveDay builds them a website (with Claude, when someone asks; nothing is built ahead of a request). The marketing pages state it as an authorized service offer. Two things only the owner can set: **(a)** whether it is included in the subscription or a separate line, and **(b)** whether any scope may be named (page count, a domain, a redesign later). Provisional default while this is open: **included in the subscription, no scope named, no turnaround promised** — the same shape as the switching concierge.

## Minimum outcome to record

The price or "included", and any scope words the pages may use.
