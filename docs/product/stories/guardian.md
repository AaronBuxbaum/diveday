# Guardian

Priya and the family she books. Minors are a safety line: a release a minor signs alone does not
count. See [personas.md](../personas.md#3-priya--the-parent-booking-a-family).

### GUARD-01 A minor's release needs a parent

As Priya, my child's waiver asks for me, refuses to finish without me, and names who co-signed.

**Done when**
- A minor's release asks for a parent or guardian and refuses without one.
- The signed record names who co-signed.
- A diver who turned 18 the day after still signed as a minor.

### GUARD-02 A seat booked as under 18 asks for a parent

As the shop, a seat declared as a minor at booking sends the parent step to the release.

**Done when**
- The seat's release asks for a parent, even before a date of birth is on file.

### GUARD-03 A parent signs for the child they booked

As Priya, I sign my child's release from my own page, with no second account.

**Done when**
- The parent's page lists the child's release and signing it clears the child's blocker.

### GUARD-04 Staff can take a parent's signature on paper

As Chloe, when a parent signs on paper at the counter, I record it on my own attestation.

**Done when**
- A parent who shares the child's name can co-sign on paper and is marked as staff-attested.

### GUARD-05 Staff cannot tick a lookalike co-signer

As the shop, a same-named person is never accepted as the guardian by a quick tick.

**Done when**
- The diver record refuses a namesake co-signer and offers no tick.

### GUARD-06 A family books on one form without invented emails

As Priya, I book myself and two kids without making up an email address for each of them.
