# Brainstorm: 35 Ideas to Add Delight to DiveDay

This list explores where we can inject joy, speed, and authentic divemaster-voice copy to make the platform feel premium, state-of-the-art, and satisfying to use.

---

## Category A: The Dock Test & Wet Phone Primitives (Manifests, Boarding, Roll Call)

| # | Idea | Description | Key Principle | Effort | Impact |
|---|------|-------------|---------------|--------|--------|
| 1 | **Favorite Marine Animal Roster Avatars** | Let divers pick their favorite marine creature (e.g. 🦈, 🐙, 🐢) during booking. Render this next to their name on the manifest so crew can start a fun conversation. | Briefing Voice | S | High |
| 2 | **Rain/Water Screen Locker** ✓ | A toggle on the manifest page that disables scroll/drag gestures and locks interaction to prevent water droplets from triggering accidental boarding state changes. | Pass the dock test | S-M | High |
| 3 | **Milestone Boarding Haptics** ✓ | Pulse custom vibration sequences at boarding progress milestones (e.g., 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) so the captain feels the boarding speed without looking down. | Haptics on key moments | S | Med |
| 4 | **"Deck-Ready" Rental Summary** ✓ | Highlight a quick gear count at the top of the manifest (e.g. "Needs: 5 XL BCDs, 2 MD Fins") so crew can preload/stage the deck before divers arrive. | Perceived speed | S | High |
| 5 | **Missing Diver Face-Grid** ✓ | A grid of initials or avatars of remaining unchecked guests, making it easier to scan the physical crowd on the dock for missing faces. | Pass the dock test | S | Med |
| 6 | **Ambient Contrast Tuning Slider** | A slider allowing a captain to override auto-glare mode and manually lock full contrast during early morning dawn/dusk hours. | AAA contrast | S | Med |
| 7 | **Emergency SMS Quick-Draft** | Emergency contact numbers on the manifest feature a one-tap draft text: `"Hi [Name], this is Captain [Name] from [Shop]. Just checking in..."` | Trustworthy | S | High |
