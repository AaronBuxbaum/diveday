# Money

- **Pass-through fee** — a fixed per-diver charge a shop collects on behalf of somebody else — a
  marine-park levy, a mooring fee — and remits in full. It rides on the checkout as its own line
  item and is **not the shop's revenue**: it must not be discounted by a promotion code, and a
  refund should return the diver's share of it. Distinct from **tax**, which Stripe computes and
  owns (ADR
  [20260826-stripe-tax-is-opt-in-and-provider-owned](../../architecture/decisions/20260826-stripe-tax-is-opt-in-and-provider-owned.md)),
  and from a **deposit**, which is the shop's money held early.
- **Room charge** — an open invoice settled by billing a resort guest's room rather than taking money
  at the counter: the hotel collects it and settles with the shop monthly. It is the order's
  `collection` (`room_charge`), with `room_number` required and `room_hotel` optional, recorded from
  the order page like cash or the card machine and marked paid out of band to Stripe. The Orders
  index adds up the month's room charges per hotel for an owner or manager. ADR
  [20261009-counter-payments](../../architecture/decisions/20261009-counter-payments.md), amended
  2026-10-16.
- **Dashboard refund** — a refund somebody made outside DiveDay, usually in the shop's own Stripe dashboard. The `charge.refunded` webhook records it on the order or checkout it reverses, reconciled to Stripe's cumulative `amount_refunded`, so a refund DiveDay made itself is never counted twice. Its trail code is `stripe_dashboard_refund`. A party checkout's dashboard refund names no seat, so no seat's payment is changed: it is held as an **unattributed refund**, its amount waiting on the Orders index ("Refunds made in Stripe") until staff pick the seat it was for, each pick taking what that seat holds; it is never split on a rule, and no automatic refund runs against that checkout's seats until it is matched (issue #2293). Matching writes `dashboard_refund_matched` on the seat's activity. ADR [20261009-stripe-reversals-reach-diveday](../../architecture/decisions/20261009-stripe-reversals-reach-diveday.md).
- **Payout** — money Stripe moved from a shop’s connected account to its bank. One `stripe_payouts` row per Stripe payout, written by the `payout.paid`, `payout.failed` and `payout.canceled` webhooks and scoped to the shop holding the event’s account; a failure or a cancellation is final. After the webhook answers, Stripe is asked, once, what it carried: the span of days ("covering Sep 27 to Oct 1") and Stripe’s fees on it. Reports lists the month’s payouts and fees; the Orders index shows the latest. Informs, never moves money. ADR [20261012-payouts-reach-diveday](../../architecture/decisions/20261012-payouts-reach-diveday.md).
- **Paid after close** — a diver's card charged on a checkout DiveDay had already closed (`expired`: a stale quote retired, a reschedule, a seat that changed type). The checkout stays `expired` and no seat is marked paid from it, because its price no longer describes any seat. `booking_checkouts.paid_after_close_at` and `paid_after_close_cents` record what Stripe captured; it waits on the Orders index, with a Refund for an owner or manager, until `refunded_cents` reaches it, by that refund or a dashboard refund. No automatic refund (issue #2335).
- **Failed refund** — a refund Stripe accepted and later reported `failed` or `canceled` (`charge.refund.updated`): the card was closed, the bank sent it back, and the money is in the shop's Stripe balance again. One `stripe_refund_failures` row per Stripe refund reverses the refund on the order or checkout, and on the seat when DiveDay made that refund itself (trail code `refund_failed`). The diver shows as owed on the Orders index until a new refund brings `refunded_cents` back to where it stood. Never re-issued automatically (issue #2291).
- **Card dispute** — a diver's bank taking a charge back, or asking about it first (an inquiry). One `payment_disputes` row per Stripe dispute against one of the shop's orders or checkouts, kept current by the `charge.dispute.*` webhooks. While it is undecided the owner sees a Today row with the amount and the day Stripe stops taking evidence. It never moves money. A dispute closed `lost` is a **lost dispute**: its reversal line (`reversed_at`) shows on the order beside what was paid, and Reports' money out counts it in the month it was lost (issue #2292). Same ADR.
- **Cancellation notice** — the email a diver gets when one booking is canceled, by themselves from their trip-prep link or with a reply confirm code, or by staff from the roster (`booking_cancelled`). It names the trip and says what happened to the money in one sentence: the amount Stripe reversed, that the shop's free-cancellation window had closed, or that the shop will be in touch. A diver who never paid reads nothing about money. Not sent for a blow-out or a minimum-head-count cancellation, which carry their own message, nor to a seat with no address, nor when staff remove a seat that was not `booked` (checked in, no-show) (`src/db/booking-cancelled-notice.ts`).
- **Subscription (DiveDay billing)** — what a *shop* pays *DiveDay*: one monthly price, billed by Stripe Billing on DiveDay's own Stripe account, never through the shop's connected account (that is the shop's money from divers). One row per shop in `shop_subscriptions`, written by the billing webhook; Settings > Billing is the owner's alone. ADR [20261007-subscription-billing](../../architecture/decisions/20261007-subscription-billing.md).
- **Pricing arm** — which of the pricing test's two plans a visitor or shop is in: *flat* (one monthly price) or *percent* (a share of the card money the shop takes through DiveDay each month, optionally with a base fee). A visitor's arm is the `dd_pricing` cookie, a setup request stores it, and a shop's is `shop_subscriptions.pricing_plan`, *assigned* or set by hand as an *override* with `pnpm billing:pricing-plan`. With the test off every arm reads as flat. A subscriber keeps the numbers it was sold. ADR [20261022-pricing-ab-test](../../architecture/decisions/20261022-pricing-ab-test.md).
- **Billing standing** — the one word a shop's subscription reads as: *trialing*, *free term*, *active*, *past due*, *canceled* or *trial ended*. Derived, never stored, by `billingStanding` in `src/lib/billing/standing.ts` from the trial window, the free term and Stripe's own status. *In good standing* means trialing, free term or active; nothing gates on it.
- **Free term** — free months DiveDay grants a shop by hand (the founding offer), set with `pnpm billing:free-term <shop-slug> <last-free-day>`. The date is the last free day, inclusive, in the shop's zone. Adding a card during the trial or a free term charges nothing until the free time ends.
- **First paid month** — the instant the first non-zero invoice for a shop's subscription was paid: `shop_subscriptions.first_paid_at`, written once, also logged as `billing.first_paid_month` and recorded as the `first_paid_month` milestone in `shop_milestones`.
- **Owed** — money the shop is waiting on, in two kinds kept apart because they are chased differently: **open orders** (invoices sent and unpaid, with the oldest one's date) and **seats that sailed owing** (a booking on a departure that has left, unpaid or deposit-only, with no open order behind it, so the ledger cannot show it). Counted in the shop's own currency only. One line on the Orders index ("Owed: $1,283 on 6 open orders, the oldest from Sep 18 · $95 on 1 seat that sailed unpaid") and the Monday email's money block. `readOwed` in `src/db/owed.ts`; ADR [20261009-counter-payments](../../architecture/decisions/20261009-counter-payments.md), amended 2026-10-14.
- **Dives owed on packages** — package dives sold and not yet taken, and what they are worth at the price each was bought for: a liability, since the shop holds the money and owes the dives. Read back in time (`packageDivesOwedAt` in `src/db/report-lines.ts`), so a past month's report says what was owed at its end; only on an order the shop still holds money for. Shown in Reports' money detail, on the Dive packages page, and as the diver's own "N of M package dives left" on their record (`diverPackageBalance`).
- **Dive (package unit)** — what one entitlement of a prepaid dive package is worth: **one tank**, not one trip, so a two-tank boat spends two ([H-123](../human-decisions/H-123-package-dive-is-a-tank.md); [20260822-a-package-is-entitlements-not-money](../../architecture/decisions/20260822-a-package-is-entitlements-not-money.md)). Said where it is bought (the packages card) and where it is spent (`/ready`'s "Uses 2 of your 5 package dives, one per tank"). A count, never a balance.
- **Paid mark** — a staffer marking a seat paid or deposit paid on the roster by hand, for money taken outside DiveDay's checkout (cash at the dock, a card on the shop's own terminal). It records the amount, the method (`cash`, `card`, `other`) and who marked it on `booking_payments` and its trail, so the money reaches Reports. Marking paid with no money, or with less than the seat owes, is a write-off, an owner's or manager's alone, and it keeps any deposit taken by hand and writes its own activity line (`payment_balance_written_off`); crew record part of a fare as Deposit paid. A seat paid online or from a package takes no hand mark at all ([H-119](../human-decisions/H-119-paid-mark-amount-and-method.md)).
- **Party payment** — "Mark the party paid" on a lead's seat: one amount and one method for the lead's seat and the seats that point at it, divided by the server, each seat taking its balance owed first and never more, with the split shown before it is taken. It never writes a seat off: a payment that runs short leaves a deposit on one seat and the rest owing, and one that is too large is refused. Each seat gets its own activity line (`party_marked_paid`). A seat paid online or from a package that still owes refuses the whole party ([H-140](../human-decisions/H-140-party-payment-split.md)).
- **Desk payment** — how a shop takes money from a diver who pays on arrival: card, cash, or either (`shops.desk_payment`, `src/lib/desk-payment.ts`), or not said (null, "Don't say"). Set on dock-day settings. One sentence ("Pay by card or cash when you arrive.") follows any figure due at the shop, and a deposit's balance, on the booking form's money block, the confirmation email and `/ready`'s Pay line. Says *when you arrive*, never *at the desk*, because the boat may leave from a marina with no desk. Informs; it charges nothing and gates nothing.
- **Stale package invoice** — a dive package a diver bought on the shop's public pages (`orders.source = 'public'`) and left unpaid for more than 7 days (`PUBLIC_PACKAGE_INVOICE_DAYS`, product owner 2026-10-13). The nightly pass (`/api/cron/trip-series`) voids it at Stripe and then on the order, which reads `void`; a refusal is read back from Stripe: an invoice paid or voided meanwhile is recorded as Stripe has it, a failed read counts nothing, and one still open or uncollectible leaves the order open, stamped (`orders.auto_void_failed_at`, `auto_void_failures`), and tried again after 3 nights, and the third refusal is raised to Sentry. An invoice a staffer raised is never voided this way (`src/db/stale-package-invoices.ts`, issue #2304).
- **Payments restricted** — Stripe has turned card charges off on a shop's connected account (`account.updated` with charges disabled). `shop_stripe_accounts.payments_restricted_at` stamps the moment, once per change of state; owners and managers see a Today row and each active owner gets one email. Bookings fall back to paying at the shop until Stripe turns charges back on, which clears the stamp.
- **Checkout sale / tip sale** — a completed online checkout or a paid tip as the shop's accounting sees it: one receipt per checkout (the party lead as customer) and a tip as income on its own line, sent to QuickBooks and Zapier on the integration outbox; a checkout refund follows as a refund receipt. ADR [20261014-checkouts-reach-integrations](../../architecture/decisions/20261014-checkouts-reach-integrations.md), [H-120](../human-decisions/H-120-checkouts-reach-the-books.md).
- **Booking lead time** — how many calendar days, in the shop's zone, before a boat departure its seats were booked (a seat booked at 6 pm for the 8 am boat is a day ahead): the median, and the share booked in the last three days, per weekday over the last twelve months, counting active seats on boat departures that were not canceled. Shore days, pool sessions and course sessions are left out, because a course sells weeks ahead with its course (dive-domain review F6, N2). Said only once a weekday has 20 seats behind it, so a new shop's handful of bookings never reads as a pattern. Reports names the three busiest weekdays ("Saturday boats: half the seats sold 9 days or more ahead · 22% in the last three days"), and the last-minute deal form says it for its own departure's weekday. `bookingLeadTimes` in `src/db/reporting.ts`, `src/lib/lead-time.ts`.
- **Month-end email** — the month's report, orders and crew sheet as CSV attachments, sent on the 2nd of each month to one bookkeeper's address a shop's owner or manager set on Reports (after the export step-up). Nothing is sent until the bookkeeper confirms the inbox through a link that carries no files, and any change of address asks again. The address keeps who set it and when, shown under the field; every set, change, confirmation and removal is emailed to every owner (to the other managers or the shop's confirmed contact address when no owner can sign in), and so is every send ("Month-end files for September 2026 sent to …"); staff changes are in the activity log. While whoever set it can no longer export the shop's data, nothing is sent until an owner or manager saves it again, which is logged and emailed as a re-confirmation. Each email carries a stop link the bookkeeper can use themselves, which clears the address and tells the owners. The first files after confirming are the next 2nd's, a month is sent once, and nothing is sent for a demo shop. Erasing a diver whose address it is clears it and tells the owners, and a restore from a backup never brings it back. The same three files download as one zip from the month's report. ADR [20261016-bookkeeper-month-end-email](../../architecture/decisions/20261016-bookkeeper-month-end-email.md).
