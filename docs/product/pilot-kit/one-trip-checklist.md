# A shop said yes: the one-trip checklist

For Aaron, the day a shop replies "send it over" or "sure, try Saturday." The goal is one real departure run in DiveDay alongside whatever the shop already does, with the shop seeing who is ready and who is not. It is a try-out, not a signup: the shop keeps the tool it books with.

Every commitment here is already authorized. Say only what the live [pricing page](https://www.dive.day/pricing) says about cost, promise no date and no feature, and follow [the improvise trap](cold-email-template.md#if-they-write-back--the-improvise-a-commitment-trap). A shop that takes payment elsewhere pays nothing.

## Before the trip

1. **Reply within the hour,** as a person, in the same thread. Thank them and ask for one thing: the passenger list for the Saturday trip they chose, as a spreadsheet, a copy from their booking tool's manifest, or a photo of the paper list. Name, email, and phone are enough.
2. **Write the call note** in the [first-call script](first-call-script.md#within-ten-minutes-of-hanging-up)'s format, and update the shop's row in the list kept outside the repo.
3. **Set up the shop and the departure.** Shops are set up by hand for now (ADR 20260925-shops-are-set-up-by-hand). Use their real name, boat, and departure time. If a [preview](preview-links.md) was already sent, build the real shop, not a second preview.
4. **Add the divers.** Paste the list onto the trip, check the preview of what was read, and fix the few rows that matched a diver already on file. A guest with no email and no phone gets a link or QR code to hand over at the dock.
5. **Check that the links went out.** The landing screen after adding guests says who was reached and by what. Open one link yourself, as a diver, and read it the way a guest would.
6. **Send the shop their view.** Give the owner the trip page, readiness first: who is ready, who is not, and why. Tell them plainly that guests with no contact are on the page too, with the link to hand over.

## What the shop sees

- The ready list: who has signed the waiver, shown a certification card, and given rental sizes, and who has not, with the reason.
- The boat list, printable.
- The head count at the dock, and again after each dive.

Do not describe anything the shop has not seen on this trip. If a shop asks about a feature that is not built or a number that does not exist yet, "not yet, and I'll write it down" is the answer.

## The morning of the trip

- Check readiness an hour before and again at the dock. Message the shop the one or two names still missing, not a report.
- **Paper runs alongside, always.** The shop keeps its printed list and its usual count; DiveDay never replaces either on the first trip. A missing, stale, or offline device copy is never a boarding source ([run sheet](v-02-field-test-run-sheet.md)).
- Do not claim the count works with no signal, or that the product has been on a boat, beyond what the [offline line](cold-email-template.md#the-offline-line--removed-from-the-first-email-2026-11-11) allows.
- Watch for what breaks, not what impresses: a guest who could not open the link, a name matched to the wrong diver, a row the paste misread.

## After the trip

1. Ask three things, in this order: what did the list get right, what did it get wrong, and would you use it on the next trip with the paper beside it.
2. Write what broke as `needs-triage` issues the same day, and the call note as before.
3. Offer the next small step only if they ask for one: another trip, or a call. Do not offer the whole shop.
4. If they stop answering, the shop goes back to the [rhythm](cold-email-template.md#the-rhythm-four-touches-then-stop)'s end: parked with the reason, never chased.
