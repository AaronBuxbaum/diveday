# 20261008-cert-check-extension — Check a card on the agency's own page from the staffer's browser

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-08

## Context

No agency offers a certification API ([H-10](../../product/human-decisions/README.md)), so [20260721-manual-certification](20260721-manual-certification.md) made certifying a manual lookup, and a card waiting for one carries a link to its agency's public lookup page (`src/lib/agency-verification.ts`). The staffer still opens the page, types the diver in, reads the answer and comes back to tap **Mark certified**. DiveDay's servers cannot do that lookup for them: the pages are the agencies', meant for a person, and PADI's sits behind the shop's own member sign-in. The staffer's browser can. Aaron asked for a browser extension that does the lookup from there, shows only when installed, falls back to the link, and is offered from Settings ([H-105](../../product/human-decisions/README.md)).

## Decision

- **A Manifest V3 extension, `extension/` at the repo root**, plain scripts with no build step and no dependencies. Chrome and Edge first. It holds host permissions for exactly the agencies it checks (SSI, NAUI, SDI, GUE, CMAS, and the PADI Pros' Site). Its content script runs only on DiveDay's staff pages (`https://dive.day/shop/*`), and the service worker answers only the top frame of one. A localhost pattern is for local testing and is never published, because Chrome ignores the port in a match pattern.
- **The extension carries words and decides nothing.** On a request it opens the agency's lookup page in a background tab, fills the form by what each box says about itself (`extension/fill.js`), waits for the answer to settle, hands the page's text back and closes the tab. If a box the lookup needs is missing it submits nothing.
- **The verdict is the server's.** `agencyCheckAction` reloads the card and the diver from the database and `judgeAgencyPage` (`src/lib/agency-check.ts`) reads the text as records: a line with the diver's first and last names in order or as "Last, First", and the few lines after it up to the next line naming them again. A record matches only when it also carries the birth date or card number the lookup was typed with (so a namesake is not certified, and the next row's level is not read as theirs), states the claimed level in that agency's own wording, and says nothing limiting (junior, referral, student, online, restricted, a try-dive, and on a public lookup a trainer's title). A birth date written all in numbers whose day and month are both 12 or under is read in the agency's own order only (`dates` in `AGENCY_CHECKS`), so a transposed date is another diver's. Short of a match the answer says which: `namesake` (someone by this name, never with this birth date or number; their words are never shown), `limited_card`, `level_unconfirmed`, `no_record`, `unreadable`. Divemaster and instructor cards are never checked (`isAgencyCheckableLevel`) and keep their link, and a TDI card is not checked either, its recreational ratings being SDI's. A higher rating's wording contains the lower one's, so it matches the lower claim; a lower never matches a higher.
- **A match certifies the card with no second tap** (H-105), through the ordinary review: `status = verified`, the staffer whose browser ran the check as `reviewedByPersonId`, the agency's matching words as `reviewNote`, and `certifications.agency_checked_at` stamped. That stamp means **the staffer's browser reported a match**, not that the agency signed anything: it attests what Mark certified attests, with the same permission (see Consequences). A match on a pending card answers `certified`, on an imported card `confirmed`, and the staffer is told which. The check's own Undo takes all of it back, and takes back only a review a check made, on that diver. Anything short of a match writes nothing and puts the link back beside what the page showed.
- **A departure's "Check all"** (`runAgencyCheckQueue`, `src/lib/agency-check-queue.ts`) is only the one-card check, card after card, one in flight, with a pause between. One unreadable result is that diver's and is skipped; two unreadable results or unanswered requests in a row stop the run, and a signed-out agency stops it at once. The staffer can stop it, a re-run skips what the page already settled, and the page repaints once when the run ends. Each card keeps its own Undo, and there is none for the departure.
- **The app finds the extension by a marker**, `data-diveday-cert-check` on `<html>`, and talks to it with `window.postMessage`, which every browser's extensions share. `src/lib/cert-check-extension.ts` and `extension/protocol.js` are the two copies of the wire format, held together by a test.
- **Scope.** Level cards that are pending or imported-awaiting-confirm. An unsighted self-declaration keeps the sighting form: its agency is often unstated and its number is the diver's typing. Specialty and nitrox cards keep the link; a match may offer the page's other cards as pending ones (layer 3, below). PADI is checked on DiveChek since [H-114](../../product/human-decisions/README.md) (below).
- **Settings says whether this browser has it**, in Bookings & waivers, with an "Add to Chrome" link once the Chrome Web Store listing exists (`EXTENSION_STORE_URL`).

## PADI eLearning (H-106)

- **What it checks.** A course student's PADI eLearning, from the session's roster, beside the materials tick of [20261008-course-learning-materials](20261008-course-learning-materials.md). Only on a PADI course with materials, for a seat not yet ticked whose student has an email and a first and last name (`elearningCheckQuery`, `src/lib/elearning-check.ts`).
- **Where it looks.** The PADI Pros' Site (`https://pros.padi.com/`, `ELEARNING` in `extension/agencies.js`), searched by the student's email, in the staffer's own signed-in browser. The extension types the email only into a box that searches (by its type, a search landmark, or its words), never on a page with a visible password box or a sign-in, sign-up or account address, so the student's details never go into a login or invite form. It hands back only the lines near the student's email, or the page's first lines when none names it: the rest of a signed-in PADI page may be about other students. The address, the search box and the wording are a **best guess** (#2259): nobody has seen the real page yet.
- **The verdict is the server's**, as for cards. `elearningCheckAction` reloads the seat, student and course from the database, scoped by shop, and `judgeElearningPage` finds one record carrying the student's name and email, then a line naming this course (every title word, and no word of a different course, so Advanced, Junior or Tec never reads as the course they contain), then its status: complete or 100%, with nothing saying in progress, started, due, a lower percent or fewer parts than all. A status is read from the course line, or the line under it when that line is a status alone. A record ends at a second email address, so neither the next student's row nor a sibling's on a shared family email is read as this student's.
- **A finished course ticks the materials with no second tap**, through `recordCourseMaterialsDone`, stamped with the staffer whose browser ran the check. A seat already ticked is left exactly as it was, and the check's Undo takes back only a tick still stamped with the staffer who pressed it. Anything short of finished writes nothing and says what PADI's page showed. No new column: the toast carries PADI's words.

## PADI DiveChek (H-114)

- **What it checks.** A pending PADI level card, from the same places as every agency (the diver record, a departure's Divers tab, Today, a departure's "Check all"), when the diver has a first and last name and a birth date on file: DiveChek is searched by name and birth date (`AGENCY_CHECKS.padi`, `src/lib/agency-check.ts`). The button says "Check with PADI"; the fallback link keeps "(member sign-in)".
- **Where it looks.** DiveChek on the PADI Pros' Site, the host the eLearning check reads (`AGENCIES.padi` in `extension/agencies.js`, `checkUrl` in `src/lib/agency-verification.ts`; the staffer's link is the same host's front door, so one sign-in serves the link, the check and eLearning), in the staffer's own signed-in browser. DiveDay never holds a PADI sign-in. The address, the form and PADI's wording are a **best guess** to be tried with #2259.
- **A signed-in page is held tighter** (`signIn` on the agency's spec). The filler finds every box and the button before typing anything. It types only into boxes that search: a search box, a search landmark, the box's own words, or a small form's search button (the form's one submit control, or a form whose every box this lookup fills; a profile form with Search beside Save is neither). It refuses a sign-in, sign-up, account or profile address (`SIGN_IN_PATH`, spelled once in `extension/protocol.js`), any page with a visible password box or an email box that does not search, and presses only the search's own button (Search, Find, Look up, Check, Chek or Verify, as whole words), never another of the page's and never a bare submit of the form. The service worker answers `signed_out`, a fixed code, when PADI shows its sign-in (a password box, a sign-in address, or another host it may not read), and hands back only the lines naming the diver and the four under each (the most the server reads as one record), plus the words saying how many divers were found; with none naming them, only the lines saying why (no result, several divers, a sign-in), at most ten.
- **The verdict is the server's, narrower than for a public page.** `judgeAgencyPage` certifies only **one diver**: DiveChek may list a row per certification, so records naming the diver are grouped by the birth date each carries, and rows sharing the typed birth date are one diver's cards. Two birth dates under the name, or a line saying "2 divers" or more, answer `several_records`; "3 results" or "3 records" does not, being possibly one diver's cards. A record ends at the first line that could be someone else's: a line naming the diver again, a line with neither a digit nor a rating's words (another diver's bare name), or a line with a date that is not the birth date and no rating's words (another diver's name and birth date), and never more than four lines under the name. Its birth date sits on the name line or the line under it, before the level; when the card has a number on file the record carries it too, though the number is never typed into DiveChek. PADI's own words for the level match (Open Water Diver, Advanced Open Water Diver, Rescue Diver; "Scuba Diver" never does). An instructor named on the row is the trainer who certified the card, not a limit. **"Junior <level>"** is the level for a diver 15 or older on the day of the check, read from the clock on the earliest calendar day anywhere, because PADI's junior ratings become the full rating at 15; under 15 it answers `junior_under_15` ("PADI shows a Junior card. Under 15, record it by hand."), and a bare "Junior" is a limit at any age. A namesake with another birth date answers `namesake` and none of their words come back.
- **Signed out.** The staffer reads "Sign in to the PADI Pros' Site first, then check again", the eLearning check's words, never "Couldn't read PADI's page", and nothing is sent to the server. A "Check all" stops on it at once, naming the diver it stopped at, because it is the agency's state and not one diver's: every PADI card after it would meet the same sign-in.
- **Terms.** DiveChek is a PADI member benefit, and PADI may read an extension filling its search as automated use. H-114 records that this was weighed and the call to build it pending a read of the Pros' Site terms and a real-page test.

## Other cards on the agency's page (layer 3)

- **What it offers.** After a match the same page often lists the diver's other ratings. `agencyListedCards` (`src/lib/agency-check.ts`) reads them, and the staffer sees "PADI also shows these certifications. Add them?" with one tick per certification, all ticked. Nitrox and the specialties a site or trip can require (`dive_specialty`), never levels: a level card's number is the agency's number for the diver, unique per agency at a shop, so a second level card cannot sit beside the one just checked, and a higher rating stays the staffer's to record.
- **Only this diver's lines.** Offers come only after a `match`, and only from the records the judge itself read as this diver's (the same window, name, birth date and card number rules), never a limited or junior one. A record is read only as far as every line is explained: the name line, the birth date, and lines made of a certification's name in that agency's words, dates, numbers, the card number on file and a few words such as "issued" (`RECORD_LINE_WORDS`). The first line with any other word ends it, so a neighbour's bare name, a neighbour's name beside a rating, another birth date on a line naming no certification, "Tec Deep Diver" or "Deep Diver Specialty Instructor" stops the reading there. On DiveChek a row may name the instructor after the certification's date, so a line is read up to the trainer's word; a trainer's word straight after a certification's name is a professional rating and ends the record.
- **A closed table per agency** (`LISTED_CARD_WORDING`): PADI's "Enriched Air Diver", SSI's "Enriched Air Nitrox", SDI's "Computer Nitrox Diver", and so on. Wording that is not in it is ignored, not guessed. GUE bundles enriched air into its level card and lists nothing. A kind the diver already has on file, in any state and from any agency, is never offered.
- **Pending, with the agency's words.** Ticked certifications land `pending`, the agency's words (this diver's record lines) as the review note, carrying the card number the matched card holds for the diver at that agency, so the confirm is the ordinary one-tap **Mark certified**. They are never certified by the check: nitrox authorizes a fill and a specialty opens a site. A nitrox or specialty confirm that brings no note of its own keeps the one the card has, so the certified card still shows what the agency's page said; its Undo clears it with the rest of the review.
- **The server believes the page text and the ticks, nothing else.** `addAgencyListedCardsAction` reads the matched card again (this shop, this diver, certified by an agency check, so not a card certified by hand or one whose check was undone), judges the posted page again and requires a match, derives the offer again, and files only if every tick names a kind that offer holds. A forged, repeated or replayed tick files nothing. The write locks the diver and re-reads what is on file, so two posts of one offer file it once.
- **Where it shows.** Under the certified card on the diver record. Under the diver's row on a departure's Divers tab, and after a "Check all" has finished, never during it: the run is not interrupted, and each row offers its own afterwards. On Today above the list, with the diver's name, because the row leaves with the card it was about. The page text stays in the staffer's browser only for as long as the offer is on screen.
- **Not built: checking a pending nitrox or specialty card with its own button** (round 2 of the ideas). The wording table would serve it, but a match there would certify a fill or a site gate from the page alone, which this layer refuses to do, and those tables have no `agency_checked_at` for the check's own Undo. It needs that decision first.
- **Known gap.** Borrowing the matched card's number assumes the agency numbers the diver rather than each card, as the glossary's **C-card** says; where an agency numbers each card, the staffer corrects it before the confirm. The real pages are still to be seen (#2259 for PADI).

## Alternatives considered

- **A server-side scraper.** Rejected: it would be DiveDay's servers hitting the agencies' pages at volume from one address, which is what H-10's "never fetches" stance was against, and it could never use a shop's PADI sign-in.
- **The extension judging the page itself.** Rejected: an extension update ships through store review, so the rule that certifies a card would be the slowest thing to fix and untestable in CI. Words in, verdict on the server, under tests.
- **A staffer's confirm tap on every match.** Aaron chose auto (H-105). The narrow match rule and Undo are what make that safe.
- **Chrome's `externally_connectable`.** Neater, but Chromium only; `postMessage` works in Firefox and Safari too.

## Consequences

- An agency that redesigns its page breaks that agency's check until the extension ships a fix. The check then answers "Couldn't read the page" and the link is still there. CI cannot test against the live pages; the filler and the judge are tested on stand-ins.
- A captcha on an agency's page stops that agency's check the same way.
- Phones never have it: mobile Chrome runs no extensions. It is a front-desk feature.
- "Checked with SSI by <staffer>" is the staffer's attestation that their browser read the agency's page, not proof the agency gave DiveDay: a staffer could post page text by hand, or a script on a DiveDay page could fake the extension's reply. Either reaches no further than Mark certified, with the same permission, and the review names the staffer either way.
- The extension sends the diver's name and what that agency's form asks for to the agency's site, as the staffer did by typing them: a birth date or a card number, both only for CMAS (`agencyCheckQuery`). Only cards still waiting for a check build a lookup.
- Publishing to the Chrome Web Store needs DiveDay's developer account, a privacy policy and the host-permission justifications: a human step.
- The eLearning check sends the student's name and email to PADI's site, which already holds both for the student it enrolled.
- The DiveChek check sends the diver's name and birth date to PADI's site, as the staffer would by typing them, and reads back only the diver's own lines. A results list with more than one diver by the name is never read as a match, even when one row would have been; a neighboring diver's row inside the excerpt ends the record when it reads as another person's (a bare name, or a name and another birth date), but a neighbor's line that carries a rating's words and no date (a name and a level on one line) still reads as part of the record above it. That is a known gap for the real-page test (#2259) to look for.
- Revisit when an agency offers an API, or if one objects to the lookup.
