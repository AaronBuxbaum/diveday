# Fictional demo diver portraits

Generated 2026-11-17 with the built-in image generation tool. The people are invented adults, not photographs of real divers; no reference images were supplied. The tool exposes no model version or random seed. The 1254 × 1254 opaque original was split into four 627 × 627 quadrants, resized to 256 × 256 and encoded as WebP at quality 82. The four files total about 46 KB. Each was inspected: centered, unobscured faces with no visible text or logos.

- Top left: `public/demo-divers/priya-sharma.webp`
- Top right: `public/demo-divers/tom-okafor.webp`
- Bottom left: `public/demo-divers/june-park.webp`
- Bottom right: `public/demo-divers/nadia-petrov.webp`

Exact prompt:

```text
Create a 2 by 2 contact sheet of four separate square realistic photographic head-and-shoulders portraits of fictional adult recreational scuba divers, suitable for small circular profile avatars. Each fills one exact quadrant, centered face, no borders, no text, no collage overlap. Top left: adult South Asian woman in her 30s, dark hair, casual dive shirt; top right: adult Black man in his 40s, short hair, casual dive shirt; bottom left: adult East Asian woman in her 30s, dark hair, casual dive shirt; bottom right: adult white woman in her 40s, brown hair, casual dive shirt. Natural daylight at a tropical marina, authentic approachable expressions, varied softly blurred backgrounds. All invented people, no logos, masks, sunglasses or text.
```

The seed assigns portraits only to freshly inserted cast IDs in demo shops, leaving thirteen customer cast members with initials. Lena's seeded age is 13, so she deliberately has no adult portrait. Existing demos are not backfilled.
