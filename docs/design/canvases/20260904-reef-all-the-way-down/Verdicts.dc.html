Every open feature idea · 36 issues · one verdict each

Eighteen drawn, eleven adopted unseen, three held, three folded, one declined.

The delight backlog (#1160) is thirty open tickets whose blocker, its own triage said, is selection, not specification depth. This is the selection. Drawn means it is on a surface board on the next page, in the place it lands. Adopt means build it from the issue as written; a picture would add nothing. Fold means it is the same feature as another row. Hold keeps the issue’s own waiting-on line. The owner’s ruling that the backlog re-triages after the first pilot boat day stands; this canvas is what that triage can read.
