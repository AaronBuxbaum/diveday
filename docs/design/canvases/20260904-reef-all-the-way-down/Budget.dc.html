The delight budget · widened, and still a budget

Reef may move, may know the hour, and may say one true thing about scale.

Reef’s three moments shipped and the product is still quiet between them. The restraint is right about status and wrong about time: nothing on a staff surface says whether it is dawn or dusk, whether a boat is out, or that today is a day the shop will remember. Eight rules below widen the budget along exactly those lines and leave every ban in place. Each names what renders when the rule is not true: nothing.

1

The water band follows the shop’s clock

Four washes for four parts of the day, read from shops.timezone. Lagoon-family and sand only: no coral, no fact, and the same 168px the band already has. Night is the one that does real work — a manager closing the day at 10 PM should not be looking at the morning.

Dawn · 5–8

warm sand over the wash

Day · 8–17

ships today

Dusk · 17–20

lagoon, deeper

Night · 20–5

lagoon-deep to sand; ink stays ink

2

Three things may move, and only these

Each is water, each moves once on a change of state, each finishes inside 600ms on --ease-out-soft, none while a field has focus, and all three are still under prefers-reduced-motion. A fourth is a new decision.

The swell across settled work. Ships (slice 13g). Draws once, left to right, when a station’s last row clears.

The water in a dial. Ships (13h and the home’s dial). Rises to the new count; at the brim it settles flat. New: the waterline carries the swell’s crest, drawn once at 1.5px in the shell colour — the roll call’s dial stays flat, because a moving line beside a head count is a distraction on the one surface where the number is the job.

The boat leaves. New. When the crew taps Underway, the station’s stage chip takes the boat and the boat drifts 12px to the right, once. It is the eighth drawing in the hand and the only one that ever moves; it appears on the home, the board and the storefront, never on the manifest.

3

One fact of scale, on the day it is true

Principle 3 declined a standing good-news line and was right. This is not standing: it is a sentence that is true once, renders in the band that morning, and never again. It takes the surface’s earned moment, so on a day that also has an all-clear line the once-a-season fact wins and the daily line renders nothing (the same rule DaySpine already keeps).

every 100th“Ben Okafor boards the 7:00 as your 400th diver of the season.” — counted from the shop’s season start, a setting with a default of the calendar year
once a season“First boat of the season.” — the morning the first departure after the season start boards
once a shop“Your first booking” and “your first boat home” — ship today; they join this table rather than living beside it
nevera week’s revenue, a comparison to last year, a streak, a rank, anything about a person who is not aboard today. A fact of scale is a count of divers or boats and nothing else.
4

A boat that is out says so, everywhere the boat is drawn

Five words the crew taps on the manifest (D20). One chip on the station, one line on the storefront, one line on every diver’s link. Renders nothing when the crew did not tap, and never a position — DiveDay does not track boats, it repeats what the crew said and when.

Boarding · 6 of 10 at the dock Out on Molasses Reef · since 7:04 On the surface · between dives Heading in · back around 10:30 Home · 10:24

The chip never carries a status: it is lagoon on every stage but Home, which is the roll call’s own word and takes the roll call’s success.

5

A mutable fact says where it came from

One small chip (D51), four words, beside any fact that can change between booking and boarding: the sea state, the sites, the meeting point, the dive record. A forecast is never dressed as an outcome. The chip is muted by default and takes the ink of its source only at the word.

Forecast, 5:00 AM an automated reading; hollow, because nobody stands behind it

Plan what the shop wrote on the trip; lagoon, because it is the shop’s intent

Crew, 7:04 AM a person on the boat said so, with the time they said it

Observed recorded after the fact, in the executed-dive log; the only one a recap may print as what happened

6

Anything a diver shares says who sees it, in the sentence that asks

D53’s grammar, drawn before counsel rules so the surfaces above could use it, and changed to whatever counsel says. Audience, then duration, then the way back. Never a checkbox with a paragraph under it.

What’s this dive for?

Your crew sees how many picked each, not who. Change it any time.

Tell the crew it’s your first time with us?

Your crew, on the day, and then it’s gone. Take it back from this link until the boat leaves.

Anything we should fix?

Just the shop. Never on the review, never public.

7

The diver’s day gets three more sentences, and a picture to keep

The boat’s-back line on the thread; the dive-day number on the postcard; the crew’s pick of the next dive with its reason. And the postcard exports as an image with no link in it (#1081), with room for one private line that never leaves the phone (D33).

“Mantis II is back at the dock.” — crew stage: Home

“Dive day № 3” — ships as visitMilestone; moves onto the postcard’s face

“Keiko said the wreck. Your Advanced card covers it.” — D35: the reason is always printed, the pick is always one, and a card that would need a level the diver lacks is never picked

8

Every ban stands

Widening a budget is not lifting it. These are the lines illustration.test.ts, the coral table and principle 9 already draw, restated so nobody reads rules 1–7 as permission.

No drawing, no coral, no motionon a manifest, a roll call, a cert check, a waiver, a payment, or beside a refusal. The manifest board on this canvas has a stage strip and a catch-up strip and not one line of the hand.
Coral stays at threethe mark’s bubble, one creature’s detail, one earned moment. The fact of scale is the earned moment on its day; it does not add a fourth.
A wash is not a statusthe band, the dial’s water and the stage chip carry no fact a word beside them does not also carry.
Nothing renders quietlya suppressed moment is not drawn small. A stage the crew did not set is not “Unknown”; it is absent.
Copy still earns its placeevery new sentence on this canvas carries a state or a consequence; the seven tells in the voice list apply to all of it.
