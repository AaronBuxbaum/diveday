What the reading may fill, and what it never touches

Fills

The four fields of the verification form: agency (matched to the app’s own agency list, or left empty), level (matched to the app’s own rungs, or left at the claim), the number, the certification date. Each marked Read until edited.

Never

The row’s status: verified is written by the tap alone, as ADR 20260824 requires, and the tap still refuses without a number. The diver’s name or date of birth, which the card may carry and the record already has. The nitrox table, whose evidence rules are its own (the hint that ships stays).

Cannot read it

Glare, a worn card, a language the reader does not know: the fields stay as they were, the sentence does not render, and the photo lands as evidence as today. No spinner longer than a beat; the photo uploads either way and the reading rides on it.

Wrong

A misread number becomes a number Dana typed over, the Read mark gone; the same check she makes today against the card in her hand. The reading is a fill, so a wrong fill costs a correction, never a wrong verification.

H-70 c · where the photo goes

No browser reads text on every phone the counter runs (Safari has no text detector), so the reading is a server call. The recommendation is Amazon Textract in the region the shop’s records already live in: the photo is already on DiveDay’s storage, the same AWS account, and Textract returns words and their boxes without keeping the image once the account’s AI-services opt-out policy is set, which is a manual step and goes in the registry of manual steps.

A card carries a name and a number, so /privacy gains one disclosure sentence naming the reader and what it is given, as it does for the push service. Declined, the photo lands as evidence as today and the four fields stay typed; the camera door still moves to the top of the form.

Why this and not a scanner

Agency cards carry no common barcode and several carry none; the eCard apps show a screen, not a code. What every card has is printed text and a person holding it. Reading the text and leaving the judgement to the person is the whole of the move.

It is also H-68’s rule applied to a camera: fill what is known, show its source, leave the last tap to a person, render nothing otherwise, never reach a safety fact. Verification is the safety fact, and the tap keeps it.
