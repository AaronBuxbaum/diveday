Who we are

Your season doesn't hang on us.

Payments run through your own Stripe account, your records download as one ZIP any day, and roll call works on a phone with no signal. Divers build DiveDay, and everything below is something you can go and try in the next few minutes.

Who's here

When you write to us, a person reads it.

DiveDay is made by a few people who dive and have built software for a living for a long time, at Google among other places. It started when a shop owner showed one of us his day: a whiteboard, a clipboard, a spreadsheet, and three apps that don't talk to each other.

Email support@dive.day and one of us reads it. There's no ticket queue in the way.

We're building the tool we'd want if we ran a shop: bookings, waivers, cert cards and the roll call in one place that works on a phone. The demo is the whole product, so the fastest way to judge us is to go run a roll call in it.
