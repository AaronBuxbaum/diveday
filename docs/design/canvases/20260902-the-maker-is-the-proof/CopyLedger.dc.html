E · The voice pass — seven tells, one example from each page

The tellsapplied to every proposed line above, and to be swept across the live pages

1 · The “X — not Y” tail: “at the desk — not at the dock”. Say the one thing.  2 · The balanced pair: “The desk clears it in the morning. The captain sees it at the dock.”  3 · The triplet for rhythm: “fast, clear, and forgiving”. Two items, or four uneven ones.  4 · The closing aphorism: “These aren't promises.” End on the fact.  5 · “It's not X. It's Y.”  6 · An adjective where a specific would do: a time, a count, a name.  7 · No “I” anywhere on a page signed by one person. And the dash budget: one per paragraph, at most.

The existing rule — read it aloud as a briefing — catches jargon and misses polish. Add a second ear: would you text this to a friend who runs a shop?
