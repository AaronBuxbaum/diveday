The surface was the problem

Your read of round 1, the same day: the core of the design is kind of ugly; evaluate the visuals themselves; there are nicer solutions that feel more Apple-like. Fair, and specific. Round 1 got the structure right and the surface wrong: it kept DiveDay's deep-sea ink, put Blue Mantis's dull green on every control, set everything on a warm grey, cut its own icons and framed no device. This page evaluates round 1's own pixels against what makes Apple's software look the way it does, decides the surface, and redraws the same six screens for all three directions on it — the three boards to the right. The one call is still H-87: A, B or C.

What was wrong

Colour first, then everything it touched. A muddy triad — navy-teal ink, forest green, khaki — and on top of it a webfont's voice, hand-cut icons, outlined cards, cramped rows and a wireframe where a phone should be.

What changes

The surface, all of it. Cool neutrals and pure white; near-black ink; one vivid lagoon-blue; the device's own face; capsules; large concentric corners; no outlines; an iPhone in the pocket. Decided here, drawn into every frame, not asked.

What does not

The floor and the three directions. The same six screens, the same fiction, the same rows and figures and words. Compare a round-2 board with its round-1 twin: nothing moved, everything changed.

What this changes in code

The token set in globals.css is rewritten rather than retuned: the six light and six dark neutrals above, one tint in two strengths and its bed, red, amber and the aboard green; sand, shell, tideline, rope, shallows, the washes and the coral tokens leave the staff surfaces. The font stack becomes the device's face with Geist as the last fallback, and next/font stops loading Geist on Apple hardware. DiveDayIcon is re-cut to the 26px, 1.6-stroke grid. Buttons become capsules in three weights. card.test.tsx, check:type-ramp, the water-band, night-palette and illustration tests are rewritten to pin this sheet. None of it waits on the pick: the surface is the same under A, B and C.

Where the recommendation stands

Still C · Figures, and the redrawn boards make the case better than the words did: the blue ring on white is the one picture of a boat filling that a shop owner will remember, and under this surface it no longer looks like a dashboard, because nothing around it is coloured. A is as calm as it was and now looks like the iPad it will run on; B's glass finally has a white page to float over. If you open one board first, open C.

How to read this page

The three boards to the right are round 1's three, redrawn frame for frame on the surface above; page 1 keeps round 1 as the record. Compare the same screen across the three before reading any prose, and compare any screen with its round-1 twin to see what the surface alone did.

What it settles

The face, the neutrals, the tint, the shape, the icons, the controls, the device and the night are decided on this page and drawn into every frame; the storefront keeps the shop's colour and face on the same shape. Refusing a row is one sentence in a session, not a lettered call.

The one call

H-87: A, B or C. Nothing else is open. The first slice, the surface, is the same under any pick and can start the day the letter is said.
