The number carries the page

Every surface opens with the one figure it exists to show — minutes to lines off, here of booked, aboard of expected, seats left, the month — large, rounded and in the same face as the words, with a ring or a bar where a count fills. Under the figure, the lists are Inset's groups and the chrome is Inset's shell. This is the instrument you asked for three times, drawn the only way Apple has ever shipped one: light, soft, one tint, and never a console.

Feels like

Weather, Fitness, Health. A summary of big figures you read in a glance, then the detail as lists. A shop owner shows it to a friend and the friend understands the morning before a word is said.

What leads

The figure. One per surface, in the page's own type at 44–56px; tiles only on the home. A ring for a count that fills, a bar for seats sold; the words beside them carry the fact, so the shape is never the only carrier.

Cost · risk

3–4 sessions. Inset's deletions plus two primitives (a Figure and a Ring) and the home's tiles. The risk is the dashboard: two figures on one surface, or a tile that reports rather than leads, and the brand's own words rule it out — so the rule is one figure per surface and tiles on the home alone.

The night

The night palette is the app's own; the ring's track is the lifted tint's wash and the arc the lifted green. A count that fills reads at depth as it reads by day.

The storefront

The diver gets a figure too: three spots on the next boat with space, and the boat that is out right now. Harbor's face on the headings, the shop's colour on the arc and the one filled button; the week is Inset's list.

Nothing below the fold changes shape: boats, reviews and the date request are groups.

What it deletes

  • everything Inset deletes: the band, the tile, the dial, the greeting, the eyebrow, the glyphs, the chips, three radii, the bed
  • the roll call's checkpoint card, its five stage chips and three-way switch above the first name — the ring and one line carry them
  • the counter's meter and the report's bars in favour of the one ring and one bar

What it keeps

  • Inset's shell, groups, rows and tab bar underneath — picking C builds A first
  • Geist, the deep-sea ink, the night palette; boat mode as a word
  • the count-first grammar the manifest and the counter already have, now shared by the home, a boat and the storefront

In code

  • Inset's list, plus Figure (a number, its unit, its sentence) and Ring (an SVG arc on --track, scaled not resized, with the words beside it); Tile for the home only, held by a test that no other route renders one
  • RollingFigure stays and drives the figure; the ring's arc follows it on --motion-base
  • one guard: one Figure per route at rest, or the surface is a dashboard
