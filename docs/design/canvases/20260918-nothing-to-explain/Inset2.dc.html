The group carries the page

Every staff surface is one or more rounded white groups on a soft ground, the way Settings, Reminders and Health are built. A boat is a group whose first row is the boat; a need is a row; a state is a word at the row's end. The page is a large title over groups, a sidebar on the desk and a tab bar in a pocket. Nothing is drawn, nothing is tinted behind text, and there is nothing on the screen a shop has not seen in an app it already owns.

Feels like

Settings, Reminders, Health. The grouped list is the most learned grammar on the planet; a shop reads it without being told how.

What leads

The list. A figure is a row's value, never a tile; the count sits at the right of the boat's row in the same type as everything else.

Cost · risk

2–3 sessions. Mostly deletion: SectionCard becomes the group, LedgerRow the row, the chrome one shell. The risk is that it is calm rather than striking: a demo says "oh, that's nice" and not "wow".

The night

The app's own night palette is the one dark — open ocean, its shell, its lifted red — with the shop's green lifted the way Harbor lifts it. Nothing else changes: the same groups, the same tap, the same words.

The storefront

Harbor keeps the shop's face on its headings and its colour on the one filled button and the current word; the rows are Inset's. A diver meets the next boat with space and one word, Book, before any scrolling.

Everything below the fold — boats, reviews, the date request — is the same grouped grammar, one section each.

What it deletes

  • the water band, the site tile, the dial, the greeting, the eyebrow, every glyph and chip, the coral on staff pages
  • three radii of four; the panel's bed; the tinted pill
  • the four trip tabs; the six-slot dock; "More"

What it keeps

  • Geist, the deep-sea ink, the night palette, boat mode as a word
  • the row's own tap as its door; the fix as the trailing verb
  • the roll call's count-first grammar and its one tap per name

In code

  • SectionCard → Group (one radius, a 1px ring, no bed); LedgerRow → Row (name · why · state · chevron); ShopPageHeader loses the eyebrow and the display size
  • ShopNav + StaffTabBar → one shell: a sidebar at lg, a five-slot tab bar below; staff-destinations.ts gains four groups
  • the trip layout's tab strip becomes groups on one page
