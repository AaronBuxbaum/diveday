The board, the blow-out, the crew

The board is the shape of the week: where the boats are, and where they are not. The kit turns the tide line on its side — seven columns, each one day as a vertical line, each departure a block that fills with its own water — and puts the add beneath the grid as one row that opens a panel already filled from the pattern of that weekday. The blow-out is the one way a booked departure leaves the board, drawn at reading width as a sentence to the divers. Staffing is the same week for people: a grid whose one loud cell is the boat with nobody in the water.

The one idea

The week is the tide line stood up. A column is a day, a block is a boat, its water is how full, its outline says next, gone, or course. What needs you is a warn mark inside the block, never a badge stack under it.

Deleted here

The standing "Usual crew" line; the per-day "+ Add" buttons (one add row, a quiet cell on an empty day); the row's ⋯ as a menu (three quiet words); the header's three buttons (Add a booking is ⌘K's; the public page is under the shop's card); the pager arrows; the "No price set" badge and the site icon (a warn mark); the unpriced notice. On Staffing: the two standing add-forms, the "Name divers see" form (the person's record), the credential badge stack (one renewal word), and "Ask for this one" as a second button. The blow-out's after-screen of tiles and a table (the same three rows, updating).

New here

The week grid drawn in the tide line's grammar (F, vertical). The add panel's second-boat row: the pattern's other departure offered as a row, never added on its own. Presence (K) on a block: the crew as chips, so the empty chip is the gap. The blow-out's message written by DiveDay for the shop to edit, with the offer as chips.

People down, days across, a cell is the boat's time. The one loud cell is the gap, in the day it lives, with its act; crew without the right to assign see "Ask for it ›" in the same cell, and the manager sees "Keiko asked" there until it is answered. A name opens the person, where shifts, away days, credentials and the name divers see are edited — nothing here is a standing form. A credential due within thirty days is one warn sentence under the name, never a gate.

What does not change

The board never removes a departure with divers on it; the blow-out does, and every diver is told before the trip leaves the board. The roll call's open count follows the departure onto the board as a danger mark. Role refusals stay sentences. The tide line carries the zone once, so the ledes do not.

Phone

The board is the stream, drawn above; the add panel opens in place under the day. The blow-out is already at reading width and folds as is. Staffing is a day list: seven groups, a row per person, the gap a warn row.

Cost and risk

Three sessions: the week grid with its blocks and the add row (the panels already exist), the blow-out page's reason, message and offer, and the staffing grid with the gap cell. The pattern-fill exists. The risk is the block's water reading as a meter of money; it is seats, and the count beside it says so.
