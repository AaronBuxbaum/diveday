U · Follow the boat

Round 3's L drew the trip's own line once and showed it to the desk and to the diver. There is a third side: the person on the dock with a coffee, the spouse who does not dive, the parent of a fourteen-year-old on her first wreck. Today they ring the shop. This board gives them a link. The pass carries "Share with whoever is waiting for you"; the link opens the trip's line in the shop's face with the stage word the crew tapped and the time they tapped it, and nothing else. The storefront's "Right now" and the dock sign carry the same line for every boat the shop has out, and a switch in Settings turns all of it off.

The one idea

The line is already true for the desk and the diver; showing it to a third person costs the crew nothing. A stage word and a time is what a phone call would have said, minus the phone call.

Composes

L (the line and the stage taps, with "as of" when stale), M (the pass carries the share line), H (the shop's face on the public page), the storefront's Right now from round 3. One public route per boat per day; no new tap.

What it never shows

A name. A count of people. A position, a map, a track. A medical fact. A boat that is late says "back by 10:30 · as of 9:12" and never "late". Off by default per shop; off, the storefront's line goes too.
