Settings — the shop's card, and every switch

Settings is where the shop describes itself once so every other page can stop asking. Today it is a forty-two-row scroll with a caption under each door and three pages hidden in the More menu; on the kit it is one rail beside one pane, and the pane opens on the shop's card: name, colour, face, mark, boats, people and the storefront in miniature, every fact a door into the page that owns it. The one thing new here is that card, and that the brand pane repaints it as you type.

The one idea

Every switch in the shop, findable in one look. The rail is the map, the pane is the switch, and the row's current value is its description.

Deleted here

The standing caption under every door row. The forty-two-row scroll (the rail carries sixteen panes; the twenty-five "Your shop" rows fold into nine). Lobby display, website embed and calendar subscriptions as destinations in the More menu (they are panes). The two queue tables at the top of "Data & integrations" (two notice rows). The security page's explanatory paragraphs. The counter card's three sentences (one row).

New here

R · the shop's card. The pane opens on it; the brand pane's colour, face, photo, badges and commitments repaint it live, by day and at night, with one sentence on contrast. K rides in the chrome as everywhere. Nothing else is new: the rows, the notice, the summary and the add row are the kit's.

The two back-office queues are two notice rows at the top of the group, present only when non-empty, each with its one act; the tables live behind them. Export is one row; the backups a summary that opens the destination. The checklist is the crew's own words as rows, the add row last, Delete the word and never one tap. Security is passkeys per device and one quiet danger act; the "how it works" paragraphs are gone. The embed is one line to paste and the widget beside it, in the shop's face.

What does not change

The emergency reference still rides on the manifest offline, and the checklist is still the crew's own lines with none of DiveDay's. Every save stays secondary; the page's one primary is "Connect Stripe" and only until Stripe is connected. Stripe, the calendar feed, the display link and the passkeys keep their confirms. The counter card stays a row whose value is the register's URL. Nothing on this board touches a diver's data or a token.

Phone

No rail; the pane is the whole map (frame 3). The shop's card compresses to a panel with one line and one door. Rows wrap once, the value beneath the name. The brand previews stack, day above night, at the phone's own width. The embed's code block scrolls inside itself; the widget preview sits beneath it.

Cost and risk

The card is a new component (one session) that reads five fields the brand pane already writes; the live repaint is the existing BrandStyle re-run on input. Folding twenty-five rows into nine panes is a route change in staff-destinations.ts and the rail (two sessions), and the More menu loses three entries. The risk is an owner who knew where "Tide window" lived; the answer is ⌘K, which finds a switch by name.
