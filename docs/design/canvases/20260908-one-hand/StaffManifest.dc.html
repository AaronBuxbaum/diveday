The manifest, the roll call, the deck, the lobby

The manifest is the head count. The desk builds it — teams, the emergency line, the dock checklist — and the boat runs it: names, one big tap each, and how many are still to call. The kit strips every one of these surfaces to boring exact words on a boat ground: a count that leads, a list that is mostly names and circles, and everything rare one deliberate tap away. The lobby board reads the same departure from across a room and never says a name.

The one idea

The head count. On the boat the count leads at 44, the still-to-call are chips, the rows are names and taps; on the desk the manifest is who dives with whom and what has to be true before the boat leaves; in the lobby it is a stage word and a count.

Deleted here

The two-button cluster per row; the per-row disclosures (a name opens the person); the checklist card (rows); the device housekeeping card (one line in the foot); the audit line per row on the desktop; the WaterLocker and contrast controls as standing cards (one line each in the foot); the numbered squares; the summary panel's four count cells (one pill and the chips); the stage strip as five buttons (five words, one bold, each a tap); the catch-up card (one notice); the buddy builder's tick lists and drag hint (Pair › on the unpaired row); the dive records on the manifest (the trip's Log tab); the emergency card (one closed row); the lobby's per-row cards, crew line and weather outlook (the meeting line, and the trip's line under the boat that is out).

New here

L on the lobby board: the current boat's own line under its row, so the room sees where it is. The buddy teams as a two-column list of rows with the guide as a presence chip (K). The dial as the glass and the figure beside it at 44. P in the lobby's empty state: the counter drawing, and nothing else.

A screen with no nav and no tap, on the boat ground, re-reading itself every minute: the shop's name and the date at 32, one row per departure in clock order at 28 — time, title, boat and sites, where to meet, then the stage word and the count. Under the boat that is out or boarding, its line, so the room knows where it is without a word. Never a name. On a day with no boats the board is the counter drawing and one sentence; that is the only drawing any of these surfaces carries.

What does not change

Commit-then-render on every tap; the 44px target and 16px critical text; boat mode's contrast and its white-by-day, dark-by-night ground; no photo, coral, drawing or motion on the manifest, the roll call, the viewer or the lobby; the stage words and checkpoint words exactly as shipped; readiness follow-up ashore, never holding a count open; the print-only souls line.

Phone

The roll call and the viewer are drawn at 390 because that is where they live; the dock is absent on both. The builder folds to one column: teams as rows, then the unpaired, then the checklist, the primary last. The lobby has no phone form.

Cost and risk

Three sessions: the builder regrouped (teams as rows, the alert as a notice, the checklist as rows), the roll call's summary panel reduced to glass, figure, pill and chips with the housekeeping in one foot line, and the lobby board's row and line. The risk is the glass reading as a decoration on a safety surface; it carries no fact the figure does not, and it never moves on the boat.
