Every message, one voice

Twenty-five kinds of message leave the app today, and every one wears the same 560px white card with a teal that belongs to no one, opens "Hi {firstName}," and ends on a mechanism. Round 3 gives each message its sender's face and one sentence: the shop speaks in the shop's mail, text and postcard; DiveDay speaks only when it acts (an invite, a welcome, a password). The confirmation is the pass, the reminder is the line, the recap is the postcard, and the thread link is the one door every message opens. What is new is the pass and the postcard arriving as mail, and the voice sheet at the foot, applied sentence by sentence.

The one idea

A message is the shop, in its colour, saying one thing and holding one door. It never explains how the app works; it says where to be, what to bring, what is still open, and it carries the same objects the pages carry: the pass, the line, the arrival card, the postcard.

The "Hi {firstName}," opener on every kind; every "we" where DiveDay speaks ("before we sail", "Text us at"); the arrows ("Track everything on your readiness page →"); the exclamation marks ("See you on the boat!", "See you in the water!", "thanks for diving {trip}!", the WhatsApp template's "Hi!"); the mechanism words (link, readiness page, track, expires, notification, confirm); the seven clipboard sentences of the reminder list (Sign your waiver · Send your shop your certification details · Check your certification level with the shop · Bring your certification card so the shop can check it · Bring your nitrox card · Send your shop your specialty details · Settle your balance); the two spellings of one act and one page ("Complete your waiver" and "Sign your waiver"; "trip prep" and "readiness"); the teal #008080, which is neither the shop's green nor DiveDay's lagoon; the three-dot mark in the head of the shop's mail; the discount code; "Please".

Cost and risk

One session for the mail shell (the shop's head, the credit foot, the four tokens from Harbor in place of #008080) and the voice pass over twenty-five bodies; one for the pass and the line as mail-safe HTML, which is the honest cost here because mail clients render neither flex nor a radial gradient the same way twice; one for the check-in push. The risk is a mail client that strips the photograph and leaves the pass as a table: the words still say where, when and what to bring, so the message survives its picture.
