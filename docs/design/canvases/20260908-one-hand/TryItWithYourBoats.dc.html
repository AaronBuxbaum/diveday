Y · Try it with your boats

Round 3 made the tide line the homepage's hero and let a new shop's first Today be the empty line with one invitation. But a shop owner reading DiveDay's homepage still has to imagine their boats on somebody else's screen. This board lets the hero take theirs. Three fields under the empty line — the shop's name, a boat, the first departure — and the hero redraws as their Today in a colour picked from their name, their boat on the line, and one door: "Open Coral Cove Dive Co.", which is the onboard form with those fields already filled. Under it, their storefront in miniature. Nothing is stored until the door is opened; a visitor who leaves leaves nothing.

The one idea

The demo is you. A homepage that draws the visitor's shop in the visitor's colour, from three words, is the product photographed with them in the picture.

Composes

S (first light), F (the tide line as the hero), H (Harbor's colour derivation, run on a suggested colour), the onboard form, the storefront's Right now. The four pictures under the hero are T, U, V and X's marketing frames.

What it never does

Look anything up: DiveDay never fetches the shop's website, listing or logo. Store a keystroke before the door. Pretend the colour is theirs: the hero says "drawn from what you typed" and the colour is changed on the first Settings visit.
