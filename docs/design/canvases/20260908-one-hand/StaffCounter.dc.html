The counter, the walk-in, the door

Cost and risk

One queue component with three groups replaces the queue, the chips track, the walk-in pages and the seat-diver panel's candidate list; the scan is the pass's code read by the counter's camera or a reader, resolved to a booking, and committed through the same check-in action the tap uses.
