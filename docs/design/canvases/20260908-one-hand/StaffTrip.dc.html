The departure, both working surfaces

A trip page is everyone who is coming, and whether they can. The roster is the page; what the dive is drops to one line that opens on request. The kit gives the departure a head — the site's photograph, the time, the boat, the crew, the count — and under it the trip's own line, the same object the diver sees on their pass and the lobby sees on its board. Prep, Log and Print are the same departure at three moments: before the boat, after the boat, and on paper.

The one idea

Everyone who is coming, and whether they can. Three groups — Needs, Aboard, Crew — and nothing else on the page until the add row. What is still open is a warn row with its one fix; who is on it is a chip.

Deleted here

The Overview tab (a disclosure); the filter chips; the per-row Details caret; the Activity and Promote footer rows (the deal is a row); the Guests route (a redirect); the About panel standing open; the change-ledger card on the staff side (it is the diver's); the water band behind the header (the photo band is the head); the capacity ring (the count is in the band); the seat-by-seat drawing and its fact links (the Needs group); the "Everyone's cleared" flash (a check line when Needs is empty); Prep's three tank tiles and its group-by control (one row; tickets are by diver, the pull list by item); the recap-note form on the Trip tab (the Log's note); the packet's "DiveDay" eyebrow (the shop's name); the departure-log page as a fourth place (Print's record, on request).

New here

L, the line, on the staff side: seven marks from check-in to back at the slip, moving on the day, drawn under the head and again on the lobby board and the diver's pass. Presence (K) on a Need: "Keiko has it", "Sal is with her". The Log as the postcard's source (N): two dives, what was seen as chips, one note to divers. Print as the shop's own document, ink only.

Paper is Geist and hairlines: the shop's name, the departure, the line as a static strip, the divers as a plain table because every column is a fact the crew reads at once, the plan, the emergency line, and when it was printed. No colour but ink; no admission requirements, on purpose. The phone keeps the head at 96, folds the line into one strip under it, scrolls the segment control, and wraps each Need under its kind; the wait list and the deal row are below the fold, and the dock keeps Board lit.

What does not change

Readiness stays Ready or Blocked and a cert stays "awaits verification" until a staffer verifies it. Send waiver completes on the row; Hand to Sal is the quiet door. The manifest, roll call and the packet's souls line are untouched here (the manifest has its own board). The recap sends once. Every remove and cancel asks twice.

Phone

Drawn above. Prep folds to the ticket rows with sizes under the name and the card as its own page; the Log's two dives and the chips stack; Print is a share sheet, the packet itself unchanged.

Cost and risk

Four sessions: the head and the line on the trip page (the panel and the disclosure exist), the roster regrouped into Needs and Aboard, the Log tab lifted from the manifest's dive records with the recap's send, and Print reset to ink. The risk is the line drifting from the manifest's stage strip: one source, the departure's schedule plus the roll call's events, feeds both.
