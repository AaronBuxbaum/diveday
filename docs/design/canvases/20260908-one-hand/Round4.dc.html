Round 4 — the app read from twelve angles, and seven things worth a "wow"

The owner's read of round 3: "Let's continue to creatively evaluate the entire app for opportunities. Think about the app from many different angles and show me a few interesting possibilities. Elegant, and it makes potential customers go wow when they see it, both in marketing and in-app." Rounds 1 to 3 read the app by surface — every route, one kit. This round reads it by angle: time, the people around the diver, the place, the sea and the sky, money, trust, memory, paper, the first minute, and DiveDay's own pages. Each angle names what the app does today and where it stops short; seven of the gaps become a possibility, T to Z, each drawn on its own board on round 3's kit, in round 3's fiction, with a marketing frame beside the in-app one. The rest of the angles are answered by an earlier lever or by nothing, and say so.

How to read this page

The angles first (section 1), because they are the audit. Then the seven possibilities (section 2), each the case, what it changes, the honest risk, the cost. Then the marketing thread (section 3): every possibility puts one true picture on DiveDay's own pages, and the homepage is redrawn from them on the Y board. Then the order and the calls.

What round 4 does not do

It does not open a new vocabulary: every board is drawn from round 3's kit, and one board names one new specimen (the print sheet). It does not touch the safety bans. It does not redraw a surface round 3 drew unless the possibility changes it. It decides nothing: the ADR's decision 6 records the round and the calls are at the foot.

The rule every possibility keeps

Marketing shows the product, never a claim about it. A year card is a real shop's year; a boat you can follow is a real boat's line; a reef page is what the crew logged. So every possibility here is an in-app thing first and a marketing thing only because it is true.

2 · Seven possibilities · each one thing on top of the kit · the case, what it changes, the honest risk, the cost
Tthe shop's year

The log rolls up into the year. Reports gains one page above the month: the year as fifty-three weeks, the biggest day, the quietest month, how many days each boat was at sea, the sites by the number of times they were dived, and the sentence the year would say — "2026 so far · 2,907 divers · 262 boats out · 48 sites." The same page prints as a card the owner sends to the crew, the landlord and the marina, and, with a yes, becomes the homepage's proof.

Extends O. The entries the close-out writes are the only source; the year page reads them and adds nothing. The card is the pass's shape at 3:2 in the shop's face. Settings gains one switch: "Show our year on DiveDay's pages" (off by default).

A year card counts divers, boats and sites and never money; the owner may hide any line. A shop with three months of entries gets three months and the card says so.

1–2 sessionsafter O · the owner's wow · Board: Year
Ufollow the boat

A link for whoever is waiting. The pass carries "Share with whoever is waiting for you"; the link opens the trip's line in the shop's face — "Mantis II · on the surface at Molasses Reef · back by 10:30" — with the stage word the crew tapped, the time it was tapped, and nothing else. The storefront's "Right now" and the dock sign carry the same line for every boat the shop has out.

Extends L to a third side. One public route per boat per day, reached by the pass or by the storefront; the same stage taps move it. No new tap for the crew.

No names, no counts of people, no position, no map: a stage word and a time. Stale taps say "as of 9:12" and never guess. A shop turns the link off in Settings and the storefront's line goes with it.

1 sessionafter L · the shore's wow · Board: FollowTheBoat
Vthe living reef

The crew's log carries what was seen, and the reef page tells it. After a dive the crew taps sightings from the shop's own species list — eagle ray, green turtle, nurse shark, the goliath grouper at the wreck — in the Log tab that already prints the postcard. The site page shows "Seen this month" with dates; the storefront's site cards say "Turtles on 9 of the last 10 dives here"; the tide line carries sunrise, sunset and the moon as quiet marks, so the night dive's line says "moonrise 8:04".

Extends N's log and the field guide the site editor already has. Two columns on the crew's dive log; one read on the site page; three marks on the tide line from the sun and moon math for the shop's coordinates.

"Seen" is a record, never a promise: the page says when and how often, never "you will see". A crew that logs nothing shows nothing. Species come from the catalogue's words, so a sighting cannot be a sentence.

2 sessionsafter N · the reef page's wow · Board: LivingReef
Wgive a dive

A seat can be bought for someone else, and it arrives as a pass. On the trip page, "Book for someone else" makes the pass with the giver's line on it — "From Hannah, for your birthday" — and a claim link; the receiver claims it, signs the waiver, and it is their pass. The postcard's back and the thread's after-state gain one seat: "Bring a buddy next time," which is the share-the-boat door counted. The Ortiz party's claim links are the same shape.

Composes M, the party's claim, and the counted referral. A gift is a paid seat on a real departure with a claim link, so it is a booking, not stored value; gift cards stay parked. The giver sees the seat claimed on their own thread.

The receiver's readiness is the receiver's: a gift never clears a cert or a waiver. A gift for a departure that blows out is refunded to the giver, and the receiver's link says so plainly.

2 sessionsafter M · the friend's wow · Board: GiveADive
Xthe shop on paper

Everything the shop prints comes from one register, in its face. Settings gains Print: the dock sign (A3, "Mantis II leaves at 7:00 · scan to check in or to follow the boat"), the boat card (the manifest's laminated fallback, day and night), the window sticker ("Book a seat with your phone"), the paper pass for a diver without a phone, the site briefing card, and the year poster. Every sheet is the kit at print size with the shop's colour and a code that opens the right door.

Extends the print packet the trip already has and the pass. One print sheet specimen joins the kit. Nothing new is stored; every sheet reads what its page reads.

Paper is not live: every sheet carries the date it was printed and the boat card says "the app is the record". Never a medical fact on paper beyond what the printed manifest carries today.

2 sessionsPDF from the kit · the dock's wow · Board: OnPaper
Ytry it with your boats

The homepage's hero becomes the visitor's shop. Three fields under the tide line — the shop's name, a boat, a departure time — and the hero redraws as their Today in a colour picked from their name, with their boat on the line and "Open Coral Cove Dive Co." as the onboard door with the fields already filled. Under it, their storefront in miniature. First light (S) begins on the homepage.

Extends S and round 3's homepage. The onboard form loses three fields because the hero asked them. Nothing is stored until the door is opened; a visitor who leaves leaves nothing.

The hero says "drawn from what you typed"; DiveDay looks nothing up and never scrapes a site. The colour is a suggestion the shop changes on its first Settings visit.

1–2 sessionsafter S · the first minute's wow · Board: TryItWithYourBoats
Zthe diver's shelf

A diver has one place at the shop. The pass's link and the postcard's link both lead to the shelf: every dive with the shop as postcards on a rail, the cert on file and whether it is verified, the waiver's date, the sizes the shop has, the buddy the shop knows, and one row — "Same boat, next Saturday · 7:00 Two-Tank Reef" — that books the next seat in a tap. The storefront says "Welcome back, Ravi. Tonight's your 12th." when the shelf's link is known to the phone.

Composes the thread, the recap and the diver record's diver-facing half. The shelf is the existing capability link promoted from one trip to the diver at the shop; no password, as today. The staff diver record and the shelf read the same rows.

The shelf shows no medical answer, ever; the questionnaire is asked fresh on every waiver as today. The link is a capability, so the shelf is only as private as the phone; "Forget this phone" is on it. H-02's erasure promise reaches the shelf whole.

3 · The marketing thread · every possibility is one true picture on DiveDay's own pages · the homepage redrawn from them on the Y board
The year card

A real shop's year, with its yes, is the homepage's proof under the hero: "Blue Mantis Divers, 2026 so far · 2,907 divers · 262 boats out". No testimonial can say it better and it is a fact.

A boat you can follow

The product page shows a live boat's line from a shop that agreed, at the stage it is at now. "Where's the boat" is the sentence a shop's phone rings with; here it is a page.

A reef page that is alive

The regions pages link to real site pages with this month's sightings. The best dive-shop page on the web for Molasses Reef belongs to the shop that dives it.

The shop on paper

The about page shows the dock sign and the window sticker as photographs, because a shop that prints its own face is a shop that has switched.

The thread's rule: the homepage carries nothing that is not, or could not be, a screenshot. The Y board draws the homepage with the hero that becomes your shop and the four pictures under it.

5 · The owner's calls · H-71, round 4 · nothing here is normative until the ADR moves to Accepted
(i) Which of T–Z, in what order.

Seven possibilities, each a slice. The table above orders them by wow per session.

Recommended: U and Y first; then T; then W and Z; then V and X.

(j) The public boat.

Whether a boat's line may be public by link and on the storefront, with a stage word and a time only, off by default per shop.

Recommended: yes, off by default.

(k) The homepage's proof.

Whether DiveDay's own pages may show a real shop's year card and a real boat's line, each with that shop's yes in Settings.

Recommended: yes, with the yes.

(l) The gift.

Whether a seat may be bought for someone else as a pass with a claim link, refunded to the giver on a blow-out; and whether the counted buddy seat rides on the postcard.

Recommended: yes to both; gift cards stay parked.

(m) The shelf.

Whether the capability link is promoted from one trip to the diver at the shop, with "Forget this phone" and no medical answer on it.

Recommended: yes, with security review.

(n) Sightings and the sky.

Whether the crew's log may carry sightings from the shop's species list and the site page may show them dated; whether the tide line may carry sun and moon marks.

Recommended: yes, and yes.

(o) Paper.

Whether Settings gains a print register and the kit a print sheet, with the six sheets drawn on the X board.

Recommended: yes.
