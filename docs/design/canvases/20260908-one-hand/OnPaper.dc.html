X · The shop on paper

The manifest prints, the arrival card prints, and the lobby board is a screen. Everything else the shop's world reads — the sign at the slip, the card taped to the console, the sticker on the door, the sheet a diver without a phone is handed — is a laminated Word document in somebody else's font. This board gives Settings a Print register: six sheets, each the kit at print size in the shop's colour and face, each carrying a code that opens the right door and the day it was printed. The dock sign, the boat card by day and by night, the window sticker, the paper pass, the site briefing card, and the year poster, which is T's card at A2.

The one idea

A shop that prints its own face is a shop that has switched. Paper is where the shop's brand meets a diver's eye first, at the dock, before any screen; the kit should reach it.

Composes

The trip's print packet, the pass (M), the follow link (U), the year card (T), H's colour and face, the site briefing from the site editor. One new specimen for the kit, the print sheet, drawn here with its rules. Nothing stored: every sheet reads what its page reads.

What it never does

Pretend to be live: every sheet carries the date it was printed, and the boat card says "the app is the record". Carry a medical fact, beyond what the printed manifest carries today. Carry a name on the dock sign or the sticker, ever.

The about page shows frames 2's two sheets as objects, not screenshots: paper photographed on a desk once the first shop prints it, the drawings until then, and the caption says which. The thread's rule holds because the sheets exist.

What does not change

The printed manifest and its numbers; the arrival card; the safety checklist's contents; the coral, photo and shop-colour bans on anything that rides a boat (the boat card wears the boat's colours, not the shop's).

The print sheet, for the kit

White ground, ink and muted from Reef, the shop's face for headings, the band in the shop's colour (the boat's on a boat), a code with a 6px quiet zone, the fold line with the print date and the storefront's address, no hairline lighter than rope at 300 dpi. A sheet is fixed pagination; the year poster is T's card at A2 and needs no second drawing.

What it costs

Two sessions after M: the Print pane, six templates on the postcard's renderer at print sizes, the print date on each, the dive-domain review of the boat card, and the specimen in the kit. The call is (o).
