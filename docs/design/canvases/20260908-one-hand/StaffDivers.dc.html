The drawer: divers, one diver, waivers, the inbox, the requests

Divers is the drawer every other page reaches into: a person, ready or not, and the one fix if not. The kit gives every list here the same row — avatar, name, the boat, the state as a sentence, a colleague's chip when someone has it, Ready or Blocked as a word — and every page the same head. The record is status, story, file: a ledger that renders nothing when clear, the story as a line of days, the file as rows that open. New on this board: the story strip on the record, the drawer drawing in the empty state (P), and an inbox and a requests page answered from the record, never from a second door.

The one idea

One person, one row, on every page that names them. What needs doing is a Status row with a button; what happened is the story; what the shop knows is the file. Waivers, Inbox and Requests are the same drawer sorted by who has not signed and who wrote.

Deleted here

The jump nav, the stat tiles, the twin certification sections, the three booking lists, the refund button on the record (money out lives on the order), the Connect-payments call to action, the merge panel as a standing card, the inbox's second "start a message" door, the requests' filter bar, the five filter chips and letter groups over Divers (the groups are the filter), the release editor as the waivers page's head (it moves to Settings).

New here

P, the drawer drawing where a new shop has nobody yet. The story as a line of days on the record. K on every row: Keiko has Grace, Sal is with Nadia. Waivers becomes who has not signed, one bar as the week's meter. Inbox rows quote the last line; Requests rows carry the fix as the door.

What does not change

Readiness is Ready or Blocked, and Blocked is never cleared in one tap. A certification awaits verification or is verified; the desk verifies, never the diver. No photograph on this board: a record, a waiver, an inbox and a request carry personal data. Delete is soft and the word is Delete; Erase is H-02's promise, typed to confirm. Merging and erasing stay owner and manager work.

Phone

Divers folds to the search control over the same three groups; a row wraps once with Ready or Blocked on its first line. The record is drawn above. Waivers keeps its button on the row and the bar under the lede; Inbox and Requests put the channel and time on the first line and the quoted sentence beneath. The empty state is the same drawing at 390.

Cost and risk

Sessions: Divers and the record (one, the row and the file), Waivers (one, the page changes shape and the release moves to Settings), Inbox and Requests (one, mostly deletion), the add form shared with the walk-in (one). Risk: today's record opens most file groups by default on desktop; the summary row hides depth, so the desk must trust that Status says everything that matters, which is the readiness engine's promise already.
