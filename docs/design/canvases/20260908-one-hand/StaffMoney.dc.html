The till and the log

Orders is the till: the month's money day by day, with the two back-office queues as notices at the top rather than tables. An order is one page with the line items, the payment and the two money acts; a new order is one form. Promo codes are the discounts a diver can type, and the last-minute deal is a row in the same family. Reports becomes the shop's log (O): the close-out writes each day's entry, the season is a strip of days, and the month's figures come from the entries — the strip and the five figures are the chart, so there is no chart.

The one idea

Money is a column, so the ledger is the one place a table is allowed, and even there each line is the row with the amount as meta. What went wrong with Stripe is a notice on the till, not a panel. The log is written by the close-out, not by a report.

Deleted here

The six bordered tiles and the bar charts on Reports, the month picker as three controls (the lede names the month), the "How's your month" title (Reports is the tab's word), the second imported-history table, the five-control filter card, the date column, the CSV as a button, the Refund on the diver record, "Monthly report" and "Connect payments" in the Orders header, the promo form as a standing card, the Paid pill (Paid is the absence of a pill).

New here

O, the log: six entries newest first, each a sentence and a small line, the 1,000th diver said once. The two Stripe queues as the one notice shape with the act on the right. The deal as a row in the promo family. The phone log with the figures two per line.

What does not change

Coral never appears on a payment surface. A refund is a second confirm, never one tap, and lives on the order. The fact of scale is a count (the 1,000th diver), never money. The imported history stays marked unverified and is never reconciled. Reports, Requests and Promo codes stay owner and manager work. The CSV keeps its tax column.

Phone

Drawn above for the log. Orders: the day groups with the amount on the first line, the two notices stacked, the chips scrolling. The order and the new order are one column already. Promo codes: the code above the sentence, Live on the first line.

Cost and risk

Sessions: Orders and the order (one: notices replace the queue tables; Refund moves here from the record), the new-order form (one: cash and link paths on one form), Promo codes (one: the form becomes the add row's sheet; the deal joins the family), Reports as the log (two: the close-out writes the entry, and the figures are read from entries). Risk: the log depends on the close-out being done every day; a day never closed is an entry with no sentence, which is honest and a little ugly.
