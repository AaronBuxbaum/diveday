T · The shop's year

Round 3 made Reports the shop's log: the close-out writes each day's sentence and the month's figures come from the entries. This board lets the log roll up once more. Reports gains one page above the month — the year as fifty-three weeks of days, the biggest day, the quietest month, how many days each boat was at sea, the sites by how often they were dived, and the sentence the year says. The same page prints as a card in the shop's face that the owner sends to the crew, the landlord and the marina in December, and, with a yes in Settings, becomes the one proof DiveDay's homepage carries.

The one idea

The year is the log read from far enough away. Nothing is entered for it; nothing is computed that the entries do not already say. An owner reads their shop's year in one scroll and keeps the card.

Composes

O (the log and the season strip), the pass's shape for the card, Harbor's face and colour, Settings' switches. Deleted here: nothing — the month page stays as round 3 drew it, and the year is above it.

What it never shows

Money, on the card or on the homepage. A diver's name. Anything an owner hid with one tap. A year with three months of entries is three months and says so.

What the card is. The pass's shape at 3:2, in the shop's photograph and face, with the year's sentence, three facts and the strip. It is one image, so it goes in a text, a mail, a frame on the wall, and, with the switch on, on DiveDay's homepage. Money never appears on it.

Every line is a door back to the year page on the shop's side; on the homepage the card is a picture and a link to the shop's storefront, nothing more. The owner hides a line by tapping it on the year page, and the card redraws.

In December the card says "2026" without "to Aug 27". A shop that opened in May gets "since May" and the strip starts there.

The fold. The sentence, the strip, the four figures two per line, then two disclosures and the print act. The strip at 390 is the same fifty-three columns at 5px, which is what the GitHub-shaped grid was drawn for.

What the year never does on a phone: scroll sideways. The sites and the entries fold into disclosures because their rows are the same rows as the month's.

What does not change

The month page, the close-out, the entries, the five figures. The card's shape is the pass's, so the print sheet X draws is the same file at A2. No new table: the year is a read over the entries.

How it folds

The desktop's four figures become two per line; the site rows and the entries become disclosures; the card prints from the phone as it does from the desk.

What it costs

One or two sessions after O: the year read, the card render (the postcard's renderer with a second template), the Settings switch, and the homepage band that reads the shops who said yes. The call is (k).
