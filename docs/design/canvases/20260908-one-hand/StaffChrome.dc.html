The chrome, the doors, the desk that answers

Everything a staff page has before it has content: the bar with the shop, the tabs and who is here; the More menu and the identity menu; ⌘K, which now answers in the row grammar rather than listing; the one notice, the one skeleton, the one error, the two doors into the app, and the phone's dock and sheet. Each frame is a slice, a few hundred pixels tall, because the page beneath is drawn on its own board.

The one idea

One shell. The chrome is 56px with the shop at the left, four tabs and More, presence and a word for search at the right; the phone folds the tabs into the dock and More into a sheet. Everything that is not a page (a menu, the palette, a notice, a skeleton, an error) is drawn once here and appears on every board unchanged.

Deleted here

Five dialog implementations (one sheet). Seven notice components (one shape, three tones). The sixteen loose ?notice= renders (the same shape, under the page header). The "Search" button's ⌘K badge box (a word). The role-switch chip in the demo bar, and the demo bar itself (Switch role moves into the identity menu). The "Language" group in the identity menu (it lives under Your account). The ⌘K footer legend, the glyph rail on every ⌘K row, and the "Boarding — today's boat" row (the tide line is that). "Welcome back" and the marketing nav around sign-in. Twenty shell-less loading.tsx files (one skeleton).

New here

Q, Ask the desk: ⌘K answers a name with the row and its fix, a day with the tide line, a unit with where it is; "Go to" is the list beneath the answer. A passkey door, with the password as the second way in. The identity menu holds the shop, the person, the role, and the two-tap sign-out. The invite landing for a new staffer is one field and one primary.

Cost and risk

One Sheet, one Notice, one Skeleton and one Menu component replace five, seven, twenty and two; the palette's answers are three renderers over the search it already runs (a person, a day, a unit) plus the tide line drawn small. The passkey door is a new auth path (WebAuthn, a credential table, a recovery story) and the biggest cost on this board; the password path stays until every staffer has one. The risk is the answer grammar: a "Send waiver" button inside ⌘K must go through the same action and the same refusals as the row on Today.
