The kit — one hand, on every surface

Direction A is the floor: one width, the eyebrow as the destination group, the title as the tab's word, one row, one add, three pills, one notice, one disclosure, one skeleton. On it ride the three levers the owner liked composed: the tide line under the chrome (F), the shop's own colour and site photographs (H), and who is here and on what (K). Round 3 adds eight small things — the trip's line, the pass, the postcard, the log, the room in the empty state, the desk that answers, the shop's card, first light — each a specimen below and each drawn on its own board. This sheet is the whole vocabulary. If a board needs a component that is not here, the board says so under "New here" and this sheet gains it, or the board loses it.

What is deleted

Five widths, nineteen eyebrows, three count spellings, five row grammars, six adds, five drawings of one state, twenty-seven summaries, three hundred skeleton bars, fifty-seven links, five notice shapes, the neutral grey capsule, coral as a wash or a meter. Every one is replaced by exactly one thing on this sheet.

What is one input

The shop's colour. Harbor already derives a brand, a deep and a tint from it with a contrast floor in both schemes; the staff app reads the same four tokens. DiveDay's own pages keep the lagoon. Nothing on this sheet has a second colour source.

What never moves

The signal colours (ok, warn, danger) and coral's bans; the 44px target and the 16px critical text; the roll call's commit-then-render; the readiness words; boat mode's contrast; the dock test. Night and boat are the same components with different tokens, drawn at the foot.

  • A fourth width, a second eyebrow source, a count joined to a label

What it holds from earlier canvases

Reef's tokens and radii (10/18/28), the 52px row, the shadow bed, the water band as the tide line's wash, Harbor's derivation and display faces, the counter's dial, the manifest's tap circle, the thread's step spine, the doors' one column.
