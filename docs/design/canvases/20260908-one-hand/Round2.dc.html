Five levers on top of A — each one thing the app has never tried

The owner's read of round 1: A is the best of the four but wants more "wow"; the instrument is hard to move around in and not warm enough; the logbook is not efficient and not what a dive shop wants to deal with; the briefing feels like too many existing apps; and all four felt too close to ideas already tried. Read against the twelve earlier canvases, that is fair — the instrument was Deck on 09-01, the paper page was Tide, and the voice, the motion, the prefill and the device were each a canvas of their own. So this page keeps A as the base (the floor and its deletions stand) and adds five levers, one per board, none of which any canvas has drawn: a place, an instrument that travels, the shop's own sea, a single page, and other people.

What "wow" has to mean here, given what the owner just said

Warm rather than cold. Efficient — fewer taps than today, never more. Recognisably not another SaaS list. And it has to land on the surface a shop lives in (the home, the counter, the manifest), not on a page a diver sees once. Every lever below is scored against those four, and each board redraws the same home for Blue Mantis on the same Thursday so they compare with A.

The five levers

E · a place

Rooms

The shop drawn as its own places: Today is the board behind the counter (a column per boat, name cards, crew magnets), Gear is the wall (tags on hooks by kind), Check-in is the counter, Divers the drawer. One card component at three sizes; one token set; every room a layout the shop already knows.

Wow: the shop recognises itself. Risk: a board on a phone is columns you swipe; skeuomorphism if it grows textures.

F · an instrument that travels

The tide line

One strip under the chrome on every staff page: the day from first light to the last boat, departures as blocks that fill as divers board, a "now" mark that moves, the next boat lit. Nothing else changes; the page beneath is A.

Wow: every page tells you how long until the lines come off. Risk: 48px of chrome on a phone; a shop with one boat a day gets a mostly empty line.

H · the shop's own sea

Their sea

Harbor comes inside: the staff app takes the shop's brand colour for its lagoon, and each departure's header carries a photograph of the site — the shop's own, or the catalogue's. The hand stays for the shop that has no photos.

Wow: it is their app, and Molasses Reef looks like Molasses Reef. Risk: photos never reach a manifest; a bad brand colour needs the derivation rules Harbor already has.

I · a single page

The one page

No tabs. The staff app is one scroll — Now, Later, the Desk, the Shop — with a rail that says where you are and search as the door to any record. A register opens as a sheet over the page and closes back to where you were.

Wow: the whole shop in one thumb-scroll on a phone. Risk: the deep registers (140 divers, 156 orders) live in sheets; the URL scheme changes; power users lose tabs.

K · other people

Together

The app knows who else is in it: Keiko on Mantis II, Sal at the counter, Dana at the desk — in the header, and on the row a colleague is acting on ("Sal is checking Diego in"). A Need can be handed to someone with one tap.

Wow: a two-person shop sees each other; the board is alive. Risk: a live layer the app does not have yet; "who is looking" is a privacy line to draw.

The recommendation, stated so it can be disagreed with: A, then F and H together, then K. F and H are cheap, compose with anything, and between them answer both halves of the brief — the tide line is the efficiency and the "not another list" in one strip, and the shop's sea is the warmth. K is the biggest wow and the biggest build. E is the most striking single board and the one to look at for the lobby display and the day-at-a-glance, but it is a second grammar beside the ledger, which is exactly what round 1 set out to delete; if E wins, it wins as the whole staff app, not as one page of it. I is the honest alternative for a phone-first shop and the one that changes the most.

What every board keeps from A

Reef's tokens, the one page width, the eyebrow as the area, titles as nouns, the one row (mark · kind · sentence · one door), the one add, the three pills, the coral bans, the voice sheet. A lever adds one thing; it never adds a second spelling of anything the floor holds at one.

The owner's call — H-71, round 2

(a) Which levers ride on A: any set of E, F, H, I, K, in an order. (b) For H: whether the shop's brand colour may recolour the staff chrome and primary action (yes recommended), and whether site photographs may appear on the staff home and trip page (yes recommended; never the manifest, roll call, waiver or payment). (c) For K: whether staff presence is shown by name to the whole shop (recommended: yes, role-wide, no history kept). (d) For E: whether the board replaces the ledger on Today or joins the lobby display only.
