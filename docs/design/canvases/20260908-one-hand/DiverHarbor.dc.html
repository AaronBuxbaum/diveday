The shopfront, in the shop's face

Every page under /s/blue-mantis is Blue Mantis Divers' own: their green, their display face, their photograph, their words on the lenses and the boats, and DiveDay as one credit line at the foot. The kit gives the storefront one column, one row for a departure and one for a course, one primary, and the trip's own line (L) where the shop is out on the water right now. The trip page keeps its pinned order — head, plan, pitch, conditions, what changed, alternates, and the form as the last word — with each piece drawn once and at one size.

The one idea

This shop is worth your dive day, and the next boat is right there. The storefront says what is happening now, what is next with space, then the week as a shelf; a trip page is one boat and one way to say yes.

Deleted here

The conservation disclaimer card (one line in the hero); the month navigator above the hero (below the shelf); the per-row metadata stack (one sentence); the "In season" tinted panel (a row in the shelf); the demo banner; the trip-type and level selects and "Hide what needs more" (the lens chips, and the requirement as a sentence on each row); the star glyphs (a figure); the boxed requirement note; the five-piece money story (one block); the sticky pill's seat count (the pill near the head); the packing section (a row in the arrival card); "Grab a spot" (no title on the form); the promo field (a quiet door); the course page's jump nav and agency tabs (the agency is a word in the row); the sizes fieldset and card number on Register (the thread asks, once there is a boat); "Powered by DiveDay" (the one credit line).

New here

L, the line, on the diver's side: under "Right now" on the storefront while a boat is out or boarding, and on the trip page at rest, every mark still to come. The species as 40px squares from the shop's guide. The known diver's welcome as an inset above the form, so a returning diver types nothing twice. A shop-namespace 404 in the shop's face that names the way back.

The widget is a window onto the shop's schedule pasted into their own site: one panel at 320 in the shop's face, the next boat with space as one sentence, one button, and the credit line; the grid and courses widgets are the same panel repeated. The host page may pass its own colour and face, and then the widget wears them. What does not change

The trip page's pinned order and its composition test; admission stays weaker than readiness, so a row never refuses a diver the desk would clear; the requirement is a sentence, never a gate at booking. The booking transaction, the Stripe redirect, the handoff email's silence (H-68), the language switcher's endonyms, the conservation words as the shop's own claim, the skip link. Payment, waiver and certification never wear the shop's colour.

Phone

The storefront is drawn at 390 beside the trip page. The trip page folds to one column with the line as the 36px strip under the head, the species three-up, the arrival rows wrapping under their kind, and the form full-bleed inside its panel with the count pill sticky at the foot in place of "Book". The catalog rows wrap the meta under the name; the course page stacks the sessions; the three narrow pages are already their phone shape.

Cost and risk

Three sessions: the storefront's head and shelf (the hero, the two panels and the line exist as components on the staff side), the trip page's head and form (the arrival card and the money block already render; the known-diver inset is a re-skin of KnownDiverPanel), and the courses pages with the widget. The risk is the line (L) drifting between the staff trip page and this one: it must be one component fed by the departure's schedule and the roll call's events, or the diver sees a boat that has not left.
