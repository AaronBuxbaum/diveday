The earned moments, and when each renders nothing

Joy is rationed to finishes. A moment fires on the act that ends something — the boat away, the diver aboard, the waiver signed, the day closed — is spent in 400 milliseconds or less, and then the surface is quiet again. Every moment below has a second rule, as binding as the first: the condition under which it draws nothing at all. That is what keeps a compliment from becoming a standing line, and coral from becoming a wash. Twelve moments in the order of a day, then the table of where coral, a photograph, motion and a buzz may go.

The one idea

A moment is a finish, never a state. It carries a check and a sentence, at most one coral detail, and at most one motion — and on a manifest, a roll call, a cert, a waiver, a payment or any boat surface it carries none of those, so the moment there is the count itself and the words that say it.

Deleted here

The standing good-news line (declined on 2026-09-04 and stays declined); the coral "Everyone's checked in" line and the 5px two-colour meter on the counter; the hand's drawing on any boat surface; the shaka glyph in the all-clear line (the bundle bans emoji; the check is the glyph); the three coral bubbles on "You're on the boat"; the check-mark emoji on "That's the paperwork done"; the sub-surface ripple on "Roll call complete"; any moment that fires on a page load rather than on a finish.

New here

Levers S (first light), M (the pass, and "You're aboard" reaching the diver through K), N (the postcard), O (the day's entry). The moments that already ship — the all-clear, the fact of scale, the season's first boat, the signed waiver, the door's terminal page — are redrawn in the kit with the emoji gone and their silence written down.

The rule for a new moment · four lines, and the test each one is
The ruleThe testWhat fails it
It is a finish, not a state.Name the act that ends something. A page load is not one; a count changing is not one until it reaches its brim.A standing line. A welcome on every visit. A meter that celebrates 70 percent.
It renders nothing when untrue.Write the silent case first and test it first. A moment with no silent case is decoration."Yesterday's charter sold out" under a diver who cannot board.
400 milliseconds or less, and safe under reduced motion.One motion, the soft ease, done before a hand can move. With reduced motion the end state is simply drawn.A ripple that outlasts the tap. Anything that moves under a finger.
A count, never money or a comparison.Say it with the day's own numbers. No revenue, no rank, no streak, no "better than last week"."Your best month yet." A coral figure with a dollar sign.
What does not change

Coral's bans hold to the letter: none on a manifest, roll call, cert, waiver or payment surface, never a wash, never a meter. Boat surfaces carry no drawing, no photograph, no coral and no motion. The roll call commits before it renders. The readiness words stay Ready and Blocked. The all-clear and the empty state stay the two lines that already ship, with the emoji gone.

Phone

Every moment here is the same at 390: the check line wraps, the pass and the postcard are 358 wide inside the phone's column, the dial keeps its 96px, the door is the one centred column it already is. The station fold on a phone waits for the finger to lift, as on the desk. Nothing on a phone is a haptic alone.

Cost and risk

Each moment is one component with two tests, the firing case and the silent case; twelve moments, twenty-four tests. The risk is a moment firing twice — a reload of the confirmed link, a second desk screen — so each fires off the record's own timestamp, once per record, never off the render. The 1,000th-diver count reads from the log's entries, which is why the log has to land first.
