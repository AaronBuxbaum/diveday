W · Give a dive

A seat today is bought by the diver who sits in it, or by a party together. It cannot be bought for someone else alone, and a diver who wants to bring a friend has a link and nothing more. Gift cards are parked in the roadmap, rightly: stored value is money DiveDay would be holding. This board finds the shape that is not a liability. On the trip page, "Book for someone else" makes the pass with the giver's line on it and a claim link; the receiver claims it, signs the waiver, and it is their pass. The giver sees it claimed on their own thread. The postcard's back and the thread's after-state gain one seat, "Bring a buddy next time," which is the counted share-the-boat door the roadmap already names. The Ortiz party's claim links are the same shape.

The one idea

A gift is a paid seat on a real departure with a claim link. It is a booking, so everything a booking has — readiness, the waiver, the blow-out's refund — is already true of it. Nothing is stored that is not a seat.

Composes

M (the pass), the party's claim link and the claim page from round 3's DiverBooking, N's postcard, the counted referral (a `booking_referrals` row and a non-secret id), Orders' rows.

What it never does

Clear a cert, a waiver or a medical answer for the receiver. Hold value past the departure: a blow-out refunds the giver and tells the receiver plainly. Show the giver the receiver's readiness beyond "claimed" and "aboard".

Give a dive

A seat on your boat, bought for a friend, arrives as a pass.

No gift cards, no balances, nothing to reconcile. A gift is a booking with a claim link; if the boat can't go, the money goes back where it came from. Included in every plan.

The pass shown is the demo shop's, drawn as a diver would hold it.

The pricing page gets one band and no new tier: a gift is a booking. The pass is frame 1's right-hand pass after the claim. The thread's rule holds; this one is the demo shop's and says so.

What does not change

The booking transaction and its capacity rule (a gift takes a seat when paid, as a party does); readiness and admission; the waiver, signed by the diver who dives; the party's claim link, which the gift reuses; the refund path.

How it folds

The gift is a phone flow first, drawn so. On the desk it is the same choice at the top of the trip page's form; on the till it is the row with its source.

What it costs

Two sessions after M and N: the choice on the form, a giver on the booking row and the giver's thread (a second capability, read-only), the gift pass template, the claim page's one new sentence, the buddy seat on the postcard reading the referral id, two rows on the year.
