Z · The diver's shelf

The thread is one trip. The recap is one trip's postcard. The diver record is the staff's. A diver who has dived with Blue Mantis twelve times has twelve links in twelve messages and no place. This board gives them one: the shelf, at the shop, reached from the pass's link and the postcard's link and never a password. Every dive with the shop as postcards on a rail, the cert on file and whether it is verified, the waiver's date, the sizes the shop has, the buddy the shop knows, and one row — "Same boat, next Saturday" — that books the next seat in a tap. The storefront says "Welcome back, Ravi. Tonight's your 12th." when the shelf's link is known to the phone.

The one idea

The capability link is promoted from one trip to the diver at the shop. The shelf is the diver-facing half of the diver record, which the staff already keep; nothing on it is new information, only a place.

Composes

The thread and its capability token (one page after booking), N's postcards, the diver record's file group, the booking form's returning-diver path, readiness. No password, as today; "Forget this phone" ends it on that phone.

What it never shows

A medical answer, ever: the questionnaire is asked fresh on every waiver as today. Another diver's anything. A price. And it never outlives H-02: erase the diver, and the shelf goes with the record.

They come back

A diver has one place at your shop, and the next seat is one tap from it.

Every dive as a postcard, the card on file and verified, the sizes, the buddy. No password, no app to install: the link on their pass. Their medical answers are never on it.

The shelf shown is the demo shop's, drawn as a diver would hold it.

The product page's band is frame 1 cropped to its first screen, from the demo shop. The sentence beside it is the one a shop owner would say to another: they come back.

What does not change

The waiver and its questionnaire, asked fresh each time; readiness; the staff diver record and who may open it; the thread as the one page after a booking (the shelf is above it, not beside it); erasure (H-02), which removes the shelf with the record.

How it folds

The shelf is a phone page first and the reading column at 1180. The storefront's welcome is the hero's sentence at both widths; the rail scrolls sideways on a phone and shows five across on a desk.

What it costs

Two or three sessions after M and N: a token scope of diver-at-shop beside the booking scope, the shelf page, the storefront's known-phone state, the sizes write, "Forget this phone", the row on the diver record.
