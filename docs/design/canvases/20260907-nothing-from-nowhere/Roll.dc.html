What rolls, and what swaps

Rolls

A count of people or seats that changed because of something that happened on this screen: the counter’s here and to-come figures, the settled group’s count, the station chip’s boarding count when the home learns of a check-in, the palette’s answer count as the query narrows.

Rolls

A countdown: the held send’s seconds. A form total as a line is added or removed in front of the person: the gear price on the booking form as rental items are picked.

Swaps

A figure’s first paint. Any figure for a reduced-motion reader. Any figure the page loaded with and nothing on the page changed: a report’s revenue, an order’s amount, a departure’s price.

Never rolls

The head count on the roll call and on the manifest’s dial, whatever H-69 c decides: a digit mid-roll is neither number for 200ms, on the figure a crew reads in glare to decide whether the boat leaves. The dial’s water keeps its rise (budget rule 2); the figure beside it swaps, as today.

Never rolls

A figure inside a refusal, or beside a field that has focus. Money that nobody changed. A fact of scale (budget rule 3), which is a sentence and is drawn once.
