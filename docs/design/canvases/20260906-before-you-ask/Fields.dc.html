Move 5 · every staff field

Type it any way.

A field takes what a person would say out loud and shows what it made of it, with what they typed one line beneath for as long as the field has focus. The value that lands is the one shown. Nothing is corrected silently.

The field shows

Where

7
7:00 AM

typed as “7”

Leaves and Back, on the add panel and the move sheet. A bare hour before noon is morning on a dive boat; “1p”, “13” and “1:00 pm” are all 1:00 PM.

sat
Sat, Sep 5

typed as “sat” · the next one

Every date field. A weekday is the next one; “tomorrow” and “9/12” land too. The day of the week is always shown beside the date, because that is the word the desk thinks in.

3055550142
+1 305 555 0142

for the shop’s country

Phone, everywhere. Ten digits in a Florida shop is a US number; a leading + is taken as written. Spaces, dots and dashes are ignored.

SHARMA, PRIYA
Priya Sharma

typed as “SHARMA, PRIYA” · kept on the record

Name, when pasted from a printed list or an agency card. The comma form and the all-capitals form are turned around; a name typed in mixed case is left exactly as typed, since “van der Berg” and “McKay” are not DiveDay’s to fix.

95
$95

in the shop’s currency

Price and deposit. “95”, “$95”, “95.00” are the same figure; the ledger keeps the cents (principle 6), the field shows the scanned form.

molasses
Molasses Reef

one match in the library

Site, boat and crew pickers match on any word, in any order. One match fills; two or more open the list with the matches first.

ml long
Wetsuit ML · long

typed as “ml long”

Rental sizes at the counter, where the diver says it and the staffer types it while they talk.

Never

A certification card number, agency or level
A head count, a seat count on the manifest, a tank pressure
A nitrox mix or a maximum depth
Any medical answer, and the emergency contact’s name

Shown exactly as typed, validated on save. A helpful guess on any of these is a wrong number on a safety document.

Three tests a forgiving field passes

1The typed text is visible beneath the result while the field has focus.
2Escape restores what was typed. Nothing is saved until the form is.
3Input it cannot read is left alone and refused on save, on the field (forms-and-controls.md).

The parsers are pure functions in src/lib with a table test per row above, in both locales, because “sat” is “sáb” to a shop in Cozumel.
