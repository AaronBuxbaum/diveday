Blocked is #B91C1C, in Geist, with the reason attached.

A shop’s gold is a beautiful colour and it means nothing at the rail. Blocked reads the same red, in the same face, with the same word, on every shop and in every language — which is the only reason a divemaster can trust it at a glance on a wet deck.

3 · The handover that never happens

The diver picks a seat, signs the waiver and pays without the address bar changing. DiveDay appears once, as a credit line in the footer, which is the whole product decision written small.

Kept from Clearwater

The staff side is untouched: one chronological spine on the shop home, the day’s stations in clock order, the closing block in the evening. Harbor changes what a diver sees, never what a shop works in.

The honest tradeoff

A shop with an ugly brand gets an ugly storefront, and we will have shipped it. And DiveDay goes nearly invisible to divers — which is the point, and a marketing cost we pay in full: no logo on the page thousands of divers book from, only a credit line in the footer.
