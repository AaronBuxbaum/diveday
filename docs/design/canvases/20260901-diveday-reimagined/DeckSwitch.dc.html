What it does, and where it stops.

What FareHarbor does well
Takes the booking and the money online, cleanly, on a page a guest finishes without help.
Puts your trips in front of hotels, concierges and resellers you would never reach on your own. DiveDay has no equivalent and won’t pretend to.
Hands you a passenger list for the day, downloadable as a CSV.
What it leaves on the clipboard
No certification record. A general booking engine has no concept of a card, an agency, or the level a site requires.
No waiver that fails closed. Nothing stands between an unsigned diver and the boarding list.
A passenger list is not a manifest. No roll call, no head count back aboard, nothing that keeps working when the signal drops.
No rental fit. Sizes stay in someone’s head, or in the spreadsheet nobody opens on a busy Saturday.
