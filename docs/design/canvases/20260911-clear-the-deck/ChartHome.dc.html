Where it stops

The sheet is the one panel, and only a boat gets one; a shop with five boats on a Saturday sees five sheets, each a glance. The desk never gets a sheet: it is not a thing that sails. If the owner wants the desk boxed too, that is Reef's card returning, and the dial's third stop shows where that ends.

What it needs

The face and the gauge from round 4's sheet, on --surface and --border; the sheet as SectionCard at one rung with no bed; the telemetry line fed by the readers that already run; the deletion of the ladder's two rungs. The home's readers do not change; its tokens do not change.

Its wow

A shop owner opens it in the morning and it is her paper — the colour her app has always been — with three boats she can read from across the desk as three rows of seats. It looks like the thing she bought, holding still, telling the truth in figures.
