The device switches the instrument to depth after dark, the way a dive computer inverts at night: the same seven tokens with the ground and the ink swapped, the two signals lifted so they hold on black, nothing else moved. What leads

Depth, not decoration

Reef's night was a palette with its own washes. Console's depth is the same instrument with the lights off: no new colour, no new shape, and the shop's rule at the top is the only thing that keeps its hue.

The evening's own lines

The log drafted from the count and the sites, the log cards scheduled, the one diver who stayed ashore and why, the Stripe call confirmed, the draft that became a seat. Facts with a time on them; nothing to celebrate and nothing celebrated.

The only amber is the deal not sent. Nothing on this page needed a new colour to be night, and nothing on it says "well done" — the figures say what happened, which on a good day is enough.

Where depth applies

Wherever the device says dark: the desk after hours, the crew's phones on the night boat, the lobby display in the evening. The roll call goes one step further to the boat scheme — black, white and the two signals at full — whatever the device says, because glare and spray do not follow a setting.

What the evening keeps

The close-out that writes the log (One hand's O), the souls-not-seats count, the open-seats debrief, the refund with its time — all of Reef-all-the-way-down's evening, as lines with figures and no swell over them.

What it needs

One prefers-color-scheme block that swaps seven tokens; the boat scheme as a third block on the roll call and the counter; no night palette of its own, no water washes, no coral at depth. The night-palette test becomes a test that the seven invert and nothing else changes.
