Three words

The staff app is three nouns — Day, People, Shop — and nothing else. Every one of today's 21 destinations, the More menu with its two groups, the phone dock and the ⌘K list fold into them. Every page beneath a word is the same list in the same grammar, and a row's own tap is its only door.

The case

A new hire learns the whole app in a sentence. Every "where is that?" has a one-word answer, so the app never asks a staffer to choose among twenty-one places — which is the choosing the current header, More sheet and palette each make her do a different way. It is Direction A's subtraction taken one level up: from the row to the map.

The honest tradeoff

Three deep trees. Shop is a drawer of nine (gear, sites, courses, orders, promo codes, reports, print, team, settings), and an owner who lives in Reports will feel it demoted two taps down. Money is not a word. A five-boat Saturday makes Day long. And it looks like a list that lines up — the wow is in the map, not the pixels.

Cost

3–4 sessions. The destination registry loses its groups and gates become per-row; three list layouts; the trip's four tabs become one page; the dock, the More sheet and the header tabs are deleted, not restyled. Composes with 4 (as its search) and 5 (as its Shop word).

What folds into which word

  • Day ← Today, the board, check-in, the walk-in counter, Took a call, staffing, the blow-out, the four trip pages, the close-out. The day's list is the board when you scroll past today.
  • People ← Divers, waivers, requests, the inbox, reviews, add a booking. A diver's record is the row's page; her waiver, her messages and her review are sections of it, not places.
  • Shop ← Gear, dive sites, courses, orders, promo codes, reports, print, team, a staffer's calendar feed, settings.

What leaves

  • The More menu, its two groups, the phone dock's sixth slot and the ceiling argument that came with it.
  • The eyebrow, the "Open …" door on every row, the "Print the day" link, the horizons panel, the water band, the tile, the dial, the chip, the pill.
  • The trip page's Overview / Guests / Manifest / Prep strip: one page, with the roll call opening as the boat's own screen.

What it keeps

  • ⌘K behind the search glyph, answering as it does since 09-06 (a name, a day, a boat — the fact and its fix).
  • Harbor on every diver-facing page; the dock test; the roll call as the one screen with no chrome at all.
  • The shop's colour as the staff app's one accent (H, which the owner liked); Geist as the only face.

Where it stops

Shop is a junk drawer, and it is honest about it: nine nouns behind one word, in the order a shop touches them. Money is a section of Shop, not a word — an owner who reads the till first will notice. Day scrolls on a five-boat Saturday; the group header is the only thing that keeps it scannable.

What it needs

One registry change, three layouts, one row. StaffNavGroup becomes the three words; gates stay per destination and hide rows, never words. The header tabs, the More sheet and StaffTabBar are deleted with their tests, not restyled. The trip layout's tab strip goes; the manifest becomes a full-screen door from the row.

Its wow

You explain DiveDay to a new hire in three words and never say "it's under More". The first morning, the app reads as a day, not a dashboard: three boats, the people on them, and what each one needs — in the order the day happens, with nothing to open.
