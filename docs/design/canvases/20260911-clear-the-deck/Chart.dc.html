What a chart refuses

A second face on one surface. A second radius. A bed at rest. A colour that is not one of the nine. A dark block on a light page. A gauge without its legend. A drawing in an empty state. A chip, a pill, a door. The greeting, the marks, the water, the moments. And one thing Console did that Chart will not: paint a page a colour the app does not have.

What it changes in code

Tokens: none added. --radius-inset, --radius-panel and --shadow-bed at rest go; the four waters, the shallows and the coral trio leave staff surfaces. Components: the face, the gauge, the telemetry line as round 4 specified them, on --surface and --border; the sheet as SectionCard reduced to one rung and no bed. Tests: card.test.tsx's ladder becomes one rung; the night-palette test is untouched; a new test holds that no staff surface paints a colour outside the nine.

What this sheet is

Round 4's sheet with the ground changed and the glass removed. Seven parts, two schemes and a word, nine tokens of which eight are already in the tree. The shortest sheet on the canvas, because most of it is already shipped.

What it is not

Not Reef with fewer moments — the ladder, the bed, the lagoon and the coral leave the staff pages. Not Console in beige — the face is the surface, the boat is a sheet, and the boat scheme is a word, which are three things Console refused.

What it needs first

The face and the gauge (round 4's two components), the telemetry line, and the sweep that deletes the ladder's rungs and the bed — in that order, on the home, which the next board draws.
