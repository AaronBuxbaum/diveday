Ask

One control. A field at the top of every screen takes a name, a day, a boat or a word — Grace, Saturday, Mantis II, waivers — and answers in place with the fact and its one fix. There is no navigation. The home is the answer to the question nobody has to type, today. Lists exist only as answers.

The case

DiveDay already holds the facts, and since 09-06 already answers in ⌘K with the fact and its fix. Navigation is a menu of twenty-one places to go and fetch what one question returns. A new staffer types what she means and gets the row, not the page the row is on. Every page deleted here existed only to hold a list somebody had to scroll to the right line of.

The honest tradeoff

Typing on a wet dock — so the boat stays a screen, never a question. Discoverability: a staffer who does not know Gear exists never asks, so the prompts under the field carry the vocabulary. Muscle memory: a page whose shape depends on the question is slow to learn by thumb. And it can read as a launcher, which the owner declined once as "existing apps" — so it is Ask over the day's answer, never Ask over a blank page.

Cost

3–4 sessions. The palette's reader exists; the answer grammar is the row; the deletions are the pages. The prompts and the empty answers need real copy. Composes with anything as its search — with Three words as the fourth control, with The week as the way into a day, with Ashore as the desk's one field.

Ask the desk

What becomes a question

  • A person — her needs, what she said, what the shop keeps. A day — what is on it, what is not, who asked for it. A boat — its next departure, its checklist, its card. A word — "waivers" is every unsigned one; "$" or "money" is the month and the two open orders; "Reg #4" is the unit and its service clock; "last month" is Reports.
  • The one question nobody types is today, and it is the home.

What leaves

  • All twenty-one destinations as places: the tabs, the dock, More, the eyebrow, every index page's header, filter row, pager and empty state.
  • ⌘K as a second thing — the field is the palette, on the page, always open.

What it keeps

  • The trip page and the diver's record as the pages an answer lands on; the boat's screen with no field on it at all; the row grammar of the 09-06 palette (a fact, then its fix), which is already tested.

Where it stops

The wet dock and the first week. On the boat nobody types, so Aboard (concept 3) or the manifest as it ships is the boat's screen. In the first week a staffer does not know the words; the Try line is the whole vocabulary and it has to stay honest and short. And a shop that wants to browse — to see Gear as a wall — gets a list as an answer, never a page.

What it needs

The 09-06 reader grown to every noun (a unit, a day's board, the month, a boat), one answer row, one empty state per kind of question ("nothing is waiting on Grace"), and the deletions: twenty-one destinations become answer kinds in src/lib/staff-destinations.ts, and the routes beneath them go with their tests.

Its wow

You type three letters and the app says what she needs and what to do about it. Nobody has ever been trained on it, because there is nothing to find — the whole shop is one question away, and on a quiet morning the answer is "nothing is waiting on you" above an empty field.
