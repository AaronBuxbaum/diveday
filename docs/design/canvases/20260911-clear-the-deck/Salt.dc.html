Salt

White ground, black ink, one hairline, and the shop's colour on exactly two things: the word you are standing on and the verb you can take. Nothing is tinted, nothing is rounded, nothing is drawn. Hierarchy is weight and size; a state is a word; the only red on the page is the word Blocked. The whole system fits on an index card.

The case

Every hue Reef added is a hue a staffer has to read past at 6 AM: sand, lagoon, coral, amber, shallows, and three washes. Salt keeps two — ink and the shop's own colour — and gives red one job. It is the surface with the least to look at and the most to read, and it prints as it stands: black on white already is the paper packet.

The honest tradeoff

It can read as cold, and as anyone's. A shop owner who chose DiveDay for its warmth sees a timetable. The warmth has to come from the words and from the one colour that is theirs — which is thin if their colour is grey. And square corners with hairlines is a look many good tools already wear; Salt is not a look you describe to a friend.

Cost

2–3 sessions. Tokens collapse to six; the radius ladder becomes zero; the panel, the bed, the band, the tile, the dial, the chip and the pill are deleted with their tests; one face replaces Geist. The cheapest surface here, because it is nearly all deletion.

What stays of Reef

  • Nothing on a staff surface. The postcard on the diver's recap keeps the hand until call (g) says otherwise; Harbor keeps the storefront in the shop's colour and face, on Salt's row and controls.

What leaves

  • Sand, lagoon, coral, amber, shallows and the three washes; the radius ladder; the bed shadow; the band, the tile, the dial, the chip, the pill, the glyph beside a word; the eyebrow; Geist.
  • The tinted current tab, the identity menu's four rows, the search button with its ⌘K badge — the field is the search.

What it keeps

  • The safety floor: 44px targets (the row is the target), 17px names on the phone, 5.3:1 for meta and 5.9:1 for the one red word, never colour alone — the two who cannot board carry a sentence. The roll call commits before it renders.

Where it stops

A shop whose colour is near-black or near-white gets a page with one hue, and Salt has nowhere else to put warmth. The counter's settled state is a word, not a filled mark — a captain used to the green disc will ask where it went, and the answer is the word Here.

What it needs

Six tokens, one face, radius zero, and the deletions. --radius, --shadow-bed, the four water washes, --accent-* and --shallows are removed rather than set; card.test.tsx stops refusing corners it no longer needs to refuse. Boat mode keeps its higher contrast on the same six.

Its wow

There is nothing to look at, so the day is what you see: three boats and the four people who need something, in black on white, with the shop's green on the one word you are standing on. It looks like it was always this simple.
