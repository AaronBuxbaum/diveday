Their ink

The staff app in one colour, and the colour is the shop's. Harbor's derivation rule, run one step further, turns the shop's brand colour into a reading ink; the same hue at half strength is the meta, at a sixth it is the hairline, at full it is the chrome and the verb. The shop's display face sits on the titles. DiveDay's own palette — lagoon, coral, sand, shallows — leaves the staff app entirely; the only other hue on the page is red, on the word Blocked.

The case

The owner liked "their sea" on 09-09 — the shop's colour coming inside. Their ink is that lever with nothing else left: not DiveDay's teal plus the shop's green plus coral plus amber, but one hue, theirs, and white. Every shop gets a different app that is still the same app, and the ugly mix goes away by construction because there is no mix. It is also the surface a shop describes to a friend: "it's in our colours."

The honest tradeoff

A whole app in one dark hue can feel heavy — a page of green text is a mood before it is a tool — and a shop whose colour is orange gets an orange office. The derivation has to fall back: a hue that cannot reach 7:1 as ink becomes DiveDay's neutral ink with the shop's colour kept as the accent, so some shops get Salt with a coloured bar. Two faces again, one of them the shop's, which H-64 declined once for Fraunces.

Cost

4–5 sessions. The derivation gains an ink step and a fallback; tokens become five derived from one input plus red; the radius ladder becomes 6; the deletions as every other surface; two faces, the shop's already loaded for its storefront.

What stays of Reef

  • Harbor, all of it — the derivation rule, the shop's face, the storefront — now on both sides of the counter. Nothing drawn; the postcard on the recap until call (g).

What leaves

  • DiveDay's whole staff palette: lagoon, coral and its deep and tint, sand, shell, tideline, rope, shallows, the four water washes, the three signal washes; the radius ladder; the bed; the band, the tile, the dial, the chip, the pill, the glyph; the eyebrow; Geist on a staff surface.

What it keeps

  • The safety floor: ink at 7:1 or the fallback, 17px names on the phone, the two who cannot board in a red sentence — red is the one hue no shop can choose, which is why it can carry Blocked under every brand. The manifest and roll call stay in neutral ink and boat mode whatever the shop's colour: a head count is never in a brand.

Where it stops

The rail and the fallback. A brand never reaches the roll call, the certs, the waiver or the payment — those keep neutral ink under every surface, so a shop's phone shows a green desk and a black boat, and the concept has to say so. And a third of real shop colours (yellows, light blues, pinks) cannot be ink; those shops get the neutral fallback, which is Salt with a coloured bar.

What it needs

One more step in deriveBrandTheme — the ink at 7:1 and the meta at 4.5:1, with the twelve-step fallback already written — five derived tokens replacing the staff palette, and the same deletions as every surface. The shop's face is already loaded for its storefront; the staff app reads it from the same setting.

Its wow

A shop signs up, picks its green, and the whole desk is in its green by the time the page reloads — not a logo in a corner, the ink itself. Two shops' screenshots side by side are two different products that work exactly the same. "It's in our colours" is the whole pitch, and it is true on every page.
