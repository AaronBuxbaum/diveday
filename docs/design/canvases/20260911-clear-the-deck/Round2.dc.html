Clear the deck, round 2 — Reef, rethought

Round 1 asked what DiveDay is organised by and drew six answers. The owner's read, the same day: the ideas land, but the surface itself — Reef, picked on 09-01 — is now the problem. So this page leaves the six concepts where they are (the shape is still call a) and redraws the surface: five candidates to replace Reef, each a different temperature, density, structure and face, each drawn on the same home for the same shop on the same morning so they compare by eye. Nothing here is normative until the owner picks.

"I like these ideas, but I think we need to go farther. I think our Reef concept needs to be rethought since I'm now finding it overbearing and ugly."

Five surfaces, one anatomy

Iwhite · black · square

Salt

Black on white, one hairline, the shop's colour on the current word and the verb. Instrument Sans. Radius 0.

For: least to look at, most to read; prints as it stands. Against: cold, and anyone's. 2–3 sessions.

IIwarm · soft · no lines

Air

Space instead of lines; a soft fill under a finger; a humanist face. Figtree. Radius 12.

For: the gentlest answer to "overbearing". Against: the least distinctive; spends rows. 3 sessions.

IIIbig · bold · two colours

Headline

Four sizes, two colours, no grey, two rules per group. The boats in the shop's colour at 28px. Schibsted Grotesk.

For: reads across a counter; unlike anything else. Against: spends height; a taste. 3–4 sessions.

IVone hue · theirs

Their ink

The shop's colour as the ink, in four strengths; its face on the titles; DiveDay's palette gone. Public Sans. Radius 6.

For: "it's in our colours"; no mix to be ugly. Against: heavy in a dark hue; a third of colours fall back. 4–5 sessions.

Vcool · compact · legible

Slate

Grey ground, one white sheet, hairlines, 44px rows, a face cut for low vision. Atkinson Hyperlegible. Radius 4.

For: densest without a table; the dock test as a face. Against: "another app". 3 sessions.

How they combine, and a recommendation

A surface is picked whole; mixing two makes a third nobody drew. The one honest composition is Their ink's colour rule under any other surface's type and structure — it is a palette rule, not a look — and the one dependency is that Their ink's fallback is Salt with a coloured bar, so picking IV means accepting I for a third of shops.

Stated so it can be disagreed with: Their ink, with Air's spacing already inside it. The owner's "overbearing and ugly" is the mix — DiveDay's teal beside the shop's green beside coral beside amber on sand — and Their ink removes the mix by removing DiveDay's colours rather than tidying them. It is the only one of the five a shop repeats to a friend, and it is the lever the owner already liked, taken to its end. If the phone matters more than the desk, Headline is the pick instead: it is the surface a captain reads from the wheel. Salt if the owner wants the cheapest honest answer; Air if the priority is that nobody can call it anything; Slate if a five-boat Saturday on one laptop screen is the job.

What no surface changes

The name and the mark. Harbor on the diver's side — the storefront in the shop's colour and face, on the picked surface's rows and controls. The dock test, and on every board the two who cannot board carry a sentence, never a hue. The coral bans, which every surface makes moot by having no coral. The roll call commits before it renders, and stays in neutral ink and boat mode under every surface, Their ink included. H-02. The claims policy.

How to read this page

This cover, then the five surfaces in a row: Salt, Air, Headline, Their ink, Slate. Each board opens with its case, its tradeoff and its cost; then its system in three cells — colour, type, shape; then the home at desktop on Three words with Ask; then the counter at 390 beside what stays of Reef, what leaves and what it keeps; and closes with where it stops, what it needs and its one wow. The fiction is round 1's, unchanged: Blue Mantis Divers, Thursday, August 27, 6:40 AM, the 7:00 Two-Tank Reef with its ten divers. Nothing here is real customer data.
