The amber in the current week is seats not yet sold on boats not yet sailed — the same amber as the night boat's five.

Where it stops

A record is only as complete as what the shop kept, so a new diver's page is mostly empty slots — which is correct and reads as such, not as a problem. Money's figures are confirmed payments only; a shop that reads bookings as revenue will find the month's number lower than it expects, and the telemetry line says why.

What it needs

The diver record's three tabs become one page with the fix as its primary; Orders, the Stripe queue, the deletion queue, refunds and Reports' month become lines and week gauges on one page. Both read from the readers that exist.

Its wow

You tap a red word on the home and land on the person, and the first button is the one that fixes it. You open Money and the month is one number on glass with the weeks as bars beneath it — and every line under it has a time.
